# RQ4 labeling standards — Kalyan (second labeler)

Written from his labeling pass, reviewed by him. Received 2026-11-19 together with his
labeled sheet.

- **Timing:** a flight landing late in the evening (around 20:00 or later) costs the
  traveler Day 1: nothing left to do, eat or unpack. 18:00 is borderline.
- **Check-in:** "too late to get to the hotel," judged from the landing time.
- **Location:** activities hundreds of km away or in another city are a mismatch; a few
  km on the same island is fine.
- **Weather:** flag when conditions make outdoor plans risky or impossible: heat
  described as "hot" (~32C) with exposed outdoor activities, heavy rain, extreme heat.
  Overcast or "monsoon" without stated severity is a judgment call.
- **Budget:** flight + hotel over the stated budget is a violation; near-but-under passes.
- **Visa:** flag it if there are fewer days before travel than the visa needs.
- **Preference:** flag when food or activities contradict the traveler (non-vegan for
  vegan), or when a stated interest isn't covered at all (no beaches on a beach trip).
