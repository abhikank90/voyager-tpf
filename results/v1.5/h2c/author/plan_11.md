## Day by day

**2027-12-15**
- Morning: No activities scheduled — flight LH arrives at 13:00. Rest and prepare for transfer.
- Afternoon: Arrive at Punta Arenas or Puerto Natales airport at 13:00. Collect luggage and arrange pre-booked transfer (shared shuttle or rental car) to Puerto Natales (~1.5 hrs). Check into a mid-range guesthouse in Puerto Natales to freshen up and gather park supplies.
- Evening: Stroll along the Puerto Natales waterfront (Seno Última Esperanza) to orient yourself. Visit a local gear shop to stock up on any missing trekking essentials — snacks, layers, trekking poles if needed.
- Notes: Pre-book your Torres del Paine park entrance (CLP ~$21,000 high season) and W Trek campsites/refugios well in advance. Puerto Natales is your last chance to buy food supplies for multi-day trekking. Confirm your boutique accommodation booking in the park for the following days.

**2027-12-16**
- Morning: Early breakfast in Puerto Natales, then board the morning shuttle bus to Torres del Paine National Park (~2.5 hrs). Arrive at park entrance, pay entry fee, and receive your park orientation map. Head to Laguna Amarga ranger station and check in.
- Afternoon: Settle into your boutique accommodation in the Central Circuit area. Take a short, easy orientation hike (1–2 hrs) around the hotel grounds or to a nearby lookout to acclimatize to the altitude and Patagonian winds. Spot local wildlife — guanacos and Andean condors are frequently seen in this sector.
- Evening: Return to accommodation for a warm dinner. Review trekking maps, weather forecasts, and plan the coming days' routes with the hotel concierge or park ranger.
- Notes: December is peak Patagonian summer — days are very long (light until ~10:30 PM). Winds can be extreme (80–120 km/h gusts are common). Always carry all layers even on sunny mornings. Hydration is essential despite cool temperatures.

**2027-12-17**
- Morning: Begin the W Trek — Day 1: Mirador Las Torres Hike. Start early (7:00 AM) from Hotel Las Torres trailhead. Hike through lenga beech forest and open pampas toward the base of the three granite towers (~9 km one way, 4–5 hrs up). The final 45-minute scramble over glacial moraine is steep but rewarding.
- Afternoon: Reach Mirador Las Torres (elevation ~900 m) and spend time absorbing the iconic view of the three granite spires reflected in the turquoise lagoon. Enjoy a packed lunch at the mirador. Begin descent back to the trailhead (~3–4 hrs down).
- Evening: Return to boutique accommodation by late afternoon. Shower, rest tired legs, and enjoy a well-earned dinner. Prepare gear for the next day's trek into Valle del Francés.
- Notes: This is one of the most demanding single days of the trip — total elevation gain ~800 m, round-trip ~18–20 km. Start no later than 7:30 AM to avoid afternoon winds at the mirador. Trekking poles highly recommended for the moraine scramble. Weather can change rapidly — pack rain gear.

**2027-12-18**
- Morning: W Trek — Day 2: Transfer by catamaran across Lago Pehoé to Paine Grande refugio (30-min scenic crossing, departs ~9:30 AM). Begin hiking into Valle del Francés from Paine Grande. The trail winds through native forest and open meadows toward Campamento Italiano (~5 km, 1.5 hrs).
- Afternoon: From Campamento Italiano, ascend into the dramatic Valle del Francés — a glacially carved amphitheater flanked by hanging glaciers, waterfalls, and jagged peaks including Paine Grande and Fortaleza. Hike to Mirador Británico for panoramic views (~4 hrs round trip from Italiano). Watch for ice avalanches calving from the glaciers above.
- Evening: Descend back to Campamento Italiano and continue to Refugio Cuernos for an overnight stay (~3 km further). Check in, shower (cold but available), and enjoy the refugio communal atmosphere with fellow trekkers.
- Notes: The catamaran schedule is weather-dependent — confirm times the evening before. Valle del Francés is arguably the most visually spectacular section of the W Trek. The hanging glaciers are active — you will likely hear and see ice calving. Refugio Cuernos has bunk-style dormitories; bring earplugs and a sleeping bag liner.

**2027-12-19**
- Morning: W Trek — Day 3: Hike from Refugio Cuernos westward along the southern shore of Lago Nordenskjöld toward Paine Grande (~11 km, 4–5 hrs). The trail offers stunning views of the Cuernos del Paine (Horns of Paine) reflected in the lake — a classic Patagonian panorama. Terrain is moderate with some rocky sections.
- Afternoon: Arrive at Paine Grande refugio by early afternoon. Rest, have lunch, and explore the immediate surroundings. Optional: short walk to the viewpoint above Paine Grande for views over Lago Pehoé and the Grey Glacier tongue in the distance.
- Evening: Overnight at Paine Grande refugio. Attend any evening ranger talks or trail briefings offered at the refugio. Prepare and pack gear for the Grey Glacier section tomorrow.
- Notes: The Lago Nordenskjöld trail section can be very exposed to westerly winds — this is often the windiest part of the W. Tighten pack straps and lower your center of gravity in strong gusts. The Cuernos del Paine views along this stretch are among the most photographed in Patagonia — keep your camera accessible.

**2027-12-20**
- Morning: W Trek — Day 4: Hike from Paine Grande to Refugio Grey along the shore of Lago Grey (~11 km, 4–5 hrs). The trail passes through lenga beech forest and open terrain with increasingly dramatic views of the Grey Glacier as you approach. Floating icebergs in the lake grow more frequent as you near the refugio.
- Afternoon: Arrive at Refugio Grey and check in. In the afternoon, join the Grey Glacier Kayaking Expedition — paddle through the icy waters of Lago Grey among floating icebergs calved from the massive Grey Glacier. This 2–3 hour guided kayaking experience (booked in advance) offers an intimate, water-level perspective of the glacier that is impossible to achieve on foot.
- Evening: Return from kayaking, warm up with hot drinks at the refugio, and enjoy the long summer evening light on the glacier. Dinner at Refugio Grey and rest.
- Notes: The Grey Glacier Kayaking Expedition must be booked well in advance through the refugio or operator (Bigfoot Patagonia is a reputable provider). Wetsuits and all equipment are provided. Minimum age and basic swimming ability may be required. The glacier is retreating — the kayaking experience offers a poignant encounter with one of Patagonia's most iconic natural features.

**2027-12-21**
- Morning: Rest and recovery day at Refugio Grey. Sleep in and enjoy a leisurely breakfast. Take a short, easy walk to the Grey Glacier viewpoint platform (~1 km from the refugio) for morning light on the ice face — the glacier glows blue in early light. Watch for condors riding thermals above the glacier.
- Afternoon: Catamaran return from Refugio Grey to Paine Grande (~30 min), then onward transfer back to your boutique accommodation in the Central Circuit area. Unpack, do laundry, and properly rest after four days of W Trek hiking.
- Evening: Celebrate completing the W Trek with a special dinner at the boutique accommodation's restaurant. Enjoy a glass of Carménère or Malbec from a Chilean or Argentine Patagonian winery.
- Notes: December 21 is the summer solstice in the Southern Hemisphere — the longest day of the year. Sunset will be around 10:30–11:00 PM, offering extraordinary golden hour light on the towers and horns. Have your camera ready for the evening. This is a well-deserved rest day — your legs will thank you.

**2027-12-22**
- Morning: Full rest and recovery morning at the boutique accommodation. Sleep in, enjoy a long breakfast, and read or journal. Optional: gentle yoga or stretching session on the hotel grounds with views of the park landscape.
- Afternoon: Join the Patagonian Wildlife and Pampas Nature Walk — a guided 3–4 hour nature walk through the open pampas and lakeshores of the park's eastern sector. Focus on Patagonian wildlife: guanacos, rheas (ñandús), Andean condors, flamingos at Laguna Azul, foxes, and pumas (rare but possible). Your guide will explain the unique Patagonian steppe ecosystem.
- Evening: Return to accommodation by late afternoon. Enjoy a relaxed evening — read, organize photos, or simply watch the sunset paint the granite towers in shades of gold and pink from the hotel terrace.
- Notes: The guided wildlife walk is best in morning or late afternoon when animals are most active, but an afternoon start works well too. Ask your guide specifically about puma tracking — December is an active month for pumas with cubs. Binoculars are essential for this activity.

**2027-12-23**
- Morning: Wake before dawn (4:30–5:00 AM) for the Mirador Las Torres Sunrise Hike. This is the iconic experience of the trip — hike by headlamp through the forest and up the moraine to reach the mirador just before sunrise (~5:30–6:00 AM in December). Watch the first rays of light turn the three granite towers from grey to gold to blazing orange in the reflection of the lagoon.
- Afternoon: Descend from the mirador after the sunrise spectacle (allow 3–4 hrs descent). Return to accommodation for a late breakfast/brunch. Rest in the afternoon — take a short walk around the hotel grounds or nap to recover from the early start.
- Evening: Relaxed evening at the boutique accommodation. Enjoy the hotel amenities — if available, use a hot tub or sauna to soothe trekking-tired muscles. Early dinner and early bedtime.
- Notes: This second visit to Mirador Las Torres is intentional — the sunrise experience is entirely different from a daytime visit and is one of the most memorable moments possible in Patagonia. Weather is the key variable; if skies are overcast, the colors will be muted but the atmosphere remains magical. Check the forecast the evening before and go regardless — conditions can change within minutes in Patagonia.

**2027-12-24**
- Morning: Christmas Eve exploration: Day hike to Laguna Azul and Laguna Larga in the eastern sector of the park. These less-visited lakes are excellent for flamingo and wildlife spotting. The trail is flat and easy (~8 km round trip) — a welcome contrast to the demanding W Trek terrain.
- Afternoon: Return from Laguna Azul by midday. Spend the afternoon at leisure — read by the window overlooking the park, organize photos and journals, or take a gentle walk near the accommodation. Visit the hotel's small library or lounge area if available.
- Evening: Special Christmas Eve dinner at the boutique accommodation. Many Patagonian lodges offer a festive set menu on December 24 — expect roasted lamb, seasonal vegetables, wine, and dessert. Enjoy the long summer evening light and the unique experience of Christmas in the Southern Hemisphere summer.
- Notes: Christmas Eve (Nochebuena) is celebrated on December 24 in Chile and throughout Latin America. The boutique accommodation may have special decorations and a festive atmosphere. This is a wonderful and unique way to experience the holiday — surrounded by one of the world's most spectacular natural landscapes.

**2027-12-25**
- Morning: Christmas Day — leisurely morning. Sleep in and enjoy a special Christmas breakfast at the accommodation. Spend the morning relaxing, reading, or taking a short walk to a nearby viewpoint to appreciate the park in its Christmas morning quiet.
- Afternoon: Final full afternoon in Torres del Paine: take a scenic drive or guided vehicle tour through the park's road network, visiting viewpoints at Lago Sarmiento, the Cuernos del Paine overlook, and Lago Pehoé. Stop for photos at the classic Cuernos reflection viewpoint — one of the most iconic images in all of Patagonia.
- Evening: Final evening in the park. Pack bags for tomorrow's departure. Enjoy a farewell dinner at the accommodation, reflecting on an extraordinary 12-day Patagonian adventure. Watch the last sunset over the towers from the hotel terrace.
- Notes: Christmas Day in the park is usually quieter than surrounding days — many staff and some facilities may have reduced hours. Confirm checkout time and transfer arrangements for the following morning. Back up all your photos and videos to cloud storage this evening.

**2027-12-26**
- Morning: Final morning in Torres del Paine. Wake early for one last look at the towers in morning light from the accommodation grounds. Enjoy a final breakfast, complete checkout, and load luggage into the transfer vehicle.
- Afternoon: Transfer from Torres del Paine to Puerto Natales (~2.5 hrs). Arrive in Puerto Natales with time for a final browse of local artisan shops — pick up Patagonian wool goods, local crafts, or a bottle of Chilean wine as souvenirs. Enjoy a final lunch in town before continuing to Punta Arenas for onward flights.
- Evening: Transfer to Punta Arenas airport (~3 hrs from Puerto Natales) or depart from Puerto Natales depending on flight routing. Reflect on an extraordinary 12-day journey through one of the world's most dramatic landscapes as you depart Patagonia.
- Notes: Allow ample transfer time — Patagonian roads can be affected by wind, weather, and wildlife crossings (guanacos on the road are common). Build in at least 3 hours buffer before any flight. The Punta Arenas airport is small but has a café and basic shops. Consider purchasing a jar of local calafate berry jam — a delicious and authentic Patagonian souvenir.
