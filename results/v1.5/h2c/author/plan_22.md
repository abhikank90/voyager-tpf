## Day by day

**2027-06-15 (Monday)**
- Morning: Arrival at Rome airport at 13:00 — no morning activities scheduled. Transfer to your boutique stay in the Vatican City / Borgo district. Allow approximately 45–60 minutes for the journey from the airport by Leonardo Express train + taxi, or a direct taxi (~50–60 EUR). Check in, freshen up, and get oriented.
- Afternoon: Leisurely neighborhood orientation walk around Borgo district and the Castel Sant'Angelo. Stroll along the Tiber River, admire the iconic Ponte Sant'Angelo with its angel statues, and get your first glimpse of St. Peter's Square from the outside — perfect for photos without crowds. Pick up a gelato at a local gelateria to ease into Roman life.
- Evening: Relaxed first dinner in the Prati neighborhood (a 10-minute walk from the hotel), a local Roman quarter beloved by residents. Explore Via Cola di Rienzo for atmosphere before settling in for dinner.
- Notes: Day 1 is intentionally light to recover from travel. Do not over-schedule — jet lag and airport logistics will take energy. Pre-book Vatican Museums tickets online tonight for Day 2 if not already done. June evenings in Rome are warm and long, so the evening walk will be pleasant.

**2027-06-16 (Tuesday)**
- Morning: Vatican Museums & Sistine Chapel (pre-booked timed entry, 9:00 AM recommended). Spend 3–4 hours exploring one of the world's greatest art collections — the Gallery of Maps, Raphael Rooms, and the breathtaking Sistine Chapel ceiling by Michelangelo. This is literally steps from your hotel, making it uniquely convenient.
- Afternoon: After the museums, visit St. Peter's Basilica (free entry, separate from the museums). Climb the dome for panoramic views over Rome (7 EUR with elevator assist, or free by stairs). Spend the afternoon exploring the grandeur of the Basilica's interior — Michelangelo's Pietà, Bernini's baldachin, and the vast nave.
- Evening: Trastevere Food & Wine Evening Walk. Cross the Tiber into the charming Trastevere neighborhood for a guided evening food walk — sample supplì (fried rice balls), cacio e pepe pasta, artichokes alla romana, and local wines while wandering cobblestone streets lit by warm lamplight.
- Notes: Book Vatican Museums tickets well in advance — they sell out weeks ahead, especially in June. Arrive 15 minutes early. Dress code applies for St. Peter's: shoulders and knees must be covered. Wear comfortable shoes — you'll easily walk 8–10 km today.

**2027-06-17 (Wednesday)**
- Morning: Campo de' Fiori Market & Cooking Class. Head to the vibrant Campo de' Fiori morning market (open until ~2 PM, best before 10 AM) to select fresh seasonal ingredients with your chef guide — local vegetables, cheeses, cured meats, and herbs. Then move to a nearby cooking studio for a hands-on class preparing classic Roman dishes such as pasta, bruschetta, and tiramisu.
- Afternoon: After the cooking class lunch, take a leisurely afternoon walk through the historic center. Visit the Pantheon (timed entry ~5 EUR), one of the best-preserved ancient buildings in the world, then wander to Piazza Navona to admire Bernini's Fountain of the Four Rivers and enjoy the lively atmosphere of street artists and cafés.
- Evening: Explore the Jewish Ghetto neighborhood for dinner — one of Rome's most historically rich and culinarily distinct areas, known for carciofi alla giudia (Jewish-style fried artichokes) and baccalà (salt cod).
- Notes: The cooking class should be pre-booked. Campo de' Fiori market is most vibrant between 8–11 AM. The afternoon walk from Campo de' Fiori to the Pantheon to Piazza Navona is an easy 1.5 km loop. June heat peaks in the afternoon — carry water and seek shade.

**2027-06-18 (Thursday)**
- Morning: Borghese Gallery Art Tour (pre-booked timed entry — mandatory, 2-hour sessions). Discover Bernini's extraordinary baroque sculptures (Apollo and Daphne, The Rape of Persephone) and Caravaggio's dramatic masterpieces in this intimate villa-museum. Arrive early for your session. After the gallery, stroll through the beautiful Villa Borghese gardens — Rome's most beloved park.
- Afternoon: From Villa Borghese, walk or take a short taxi to the Spanish Steps and the Trevi Fountain area. Toss a coin in the Trevi Fountain (best visited on a weekday afternoon rather than evening for slightly smaller crowds), then climb the Spanish Steps and explore the luxury shopping street Via Condotti — window shopping is free! Head toward Via Veneto for a classic Roman café experience.
- Evening: Return toward the hotel via Prati or stay near the Trevi area for dinner. This is a good evening for a quieter, more refined dining experience.
- Notes: Borghese Gallery tickets MUST be pre-booked — entry is strictly limited to 360 visitors per 2-hour session. Book as early as possible, ideally weeks in advance. The gallery is closed Mondays. Taxi from hotel to gallery is recommended (~10–12 EUR) to save time.

**2027-06-19 (Friday)**
- Morning: Palatine Hill & Roman Forum Archaeological Walk. Take a taxi or bus to the Colosseum area and spend the morning on an immersive archaeological walk through the Roman Forum and Palatine Hill — the ancient heart of the Roman Empire. Explore ruins of temples, the Via Sacra, the Arch of Titus, and the imperial palaces on Palatine Hill with sweeping views over the Forum below. (Combined ticket ~18 EUR; a guided tour is highly recommended for context — ~25–35 EUR extra.)
- Afternoon: Visit the Colosseum (included in the combined ticket — book a timed entry slot). Explore one of the ancient world's most iconic structures. Afterward, head to the nearby Celio neighborhood — a quiet, leafy hill with excellent views and the beautiful Basilica di San Clemente, which has three layers of history built on top of each other (free–5 EUR).
- Evening: Head to the Testaccio neighborhood for dinner — Rome's traditional working-class culinary heartland, known for offal dishes, authentic trattorias, and the famous Testaccio Market. Testaccio is approximately 20 minutes by taxi from your hotel.
- Notes: Wear sunscreen and a hat — Palatine Hill and the Forum have very little shade, and June sun can be intense. Bring at least 1.5 liters of water per person. Start as early as possible (gates open at 9 AM). The combined Forum + Palatine + Colosseum ticket must be booked online in advance.

**2027-06-20 (Saturday)**
- Morning: Free morning for personal exploration and leisure. Sleep in and enjoy a relaxed breakfast. Visit the Castel Sant'Angelo museum (steps from your hotel, ~15 EUR) — explore the former mausoleum-turned-fortress with its secret papal escape corridor (Passetto di Borgo) and panoramic rooftop terrace with some of the best views in Rome.
- Afternoon: Afternoon at leisure — revisit a favorite neighborhood or museum, do some shopping (try Via Cola di Rienzo in Prati for local boutiques, bookshops, and food stores), or visit the lesser-known but stunning Palazzo Altemps museum (part of the National Roman Museum) near Piazza Navona, housing classical sculpture in a beautiful Renaissance palace (~10 EUR).
- Evening: Farewell splurge dinner — treat yourself to a special meal at a higher-end Roman restaurant to celebrate the trip. Rome's Prati neighborhood has several excellent options within walking distance of the hotel.
- Notes: Saturday in Rome means busier markets and more street life — embrace it! The Castel Sant'Angelo is extremely close to your hotel (5-minute walk) and often overlooked by tourists rushing to the Vatican. The rooftop view at sunset is spectacular. Book the farewell dinner reservation a few days ahead.

**2027-06-21 (Sunday)**
- Morning: Final morning in Rome — attend the free Papal Angelus blessing in St. Peter's Square at 12:00 noon (the Pope appears at his window to address the crowd — a unique and moving experience, especially on Sundays). Arrive by 11:30 AM to secure a good spot. Alternatively, if not interested, take a final morning walk along the Tiber River and pick up last-minute souvenirs in Prati.
- Afternoon: Check out of the hotel and head to the airport. Allow ample time: Rome traffic on Sundays can be unpredictable, and it's advisable to leave for the airport at least 3 hours before your flight. Store luggage at the hotel until departure if your flight is late afternoon or evening.
- Evening: Departure from Rome. Arrivederci, Roma!
- Notes: Sunday morning at St. Peter's Square can be very crowded for the Angelus — arrive early and be prepared for security bag checks at the square entrance. If your flight departs before noon, skip the Angelus and head directly to the airport. Rome Fiumicino (FCO) airport requires at least 2–2.5 hours for check-in and security on busy summer Sundays.
