## Day by day

**2027-07-15**
- Morning: No morning activities — flight arrives at 13:00. Rest and prepare for landing.
- Afternoon: Arrive at Ngurah Rai International Airport at 13:00. Clear customs, collect luggage, and arrange a pre-booked airport transfer to Ubud Boutique Stay (approx. 1.5–2 hrs). Check in, freshen up, and take a gentle stroll along Monkey Forest Road to get oriented with the neighbourhood.
- Evening: Explore Ubud's vibrant night scene on Jalan Hanoman. Browse local art shops and settle into the Balinese atmosphere with a relaxed walk through the town centre.
- Notes: Keep Day 1 light to recover from travel. Hydrate well and adjust to the warm, humid climate. Exchange currency at a reputable money changer in Ubud rather than the airport for better rates.

**2027-07-16**
- Morning: Start the day with a sunrise yoga class at one of Ubud's renowned yoga studios, such as Yoga Barn or Radiantly Alive. A 90-minute morning flow class costs approx. IDR 150,000–200,000. Return to the hotel for breakfast.
- Afternoon: Visit the Sacred Monkey Forest Sanctuary (entry approx. IDR 80,000). Wander through the ancient temple grounds and lush jungle paths. Afterward, explore the Ubud Art Market (Pasar Seni Ubud) for handmade crafts, sarongs, and souvenirs.
- Evening: Attend a traditional Kecak Fire Dance performance at Ubud Palace (Puri Saren Agung) — tickets approx. IDR 100,000–150,000. A mesmerising cultural experience set in an open-air royal courtyard.
- Notes: Book the Kecak Dance in advance or ask the hotel concierge. Wear a sarong to enter temple areas — many can be rented at the entrance.

**2027-07-17**
- Morning: Rent a bicycle or join a guided cycling tour through Ubud's countryside. Ride through rice paddies, traditional villages, and past the Campuhan Ridge Walk trail. A guided cycling tour costs approx. IDR 250,000–350,000 including breakfast.
- Afternoon: Visit Tirta Empul Temple — a sacred Hindu water temple famous for its purification rituals in holy spring water (entry approx. IDR 50,000). Participate respectfully in the melukat purification ritual if comfortable.
- Evening: Relax with a traditional Balinese massage at a reputable spa in Ubud, such as COMO Shambhala or Taksu Spa. A 60-minute massage costs IDR 200,000–350,000.
- Notes: Bring a change of clothes and a waterproof bag if participating in the Tirta Empul water ritual. Light rain is possible in the afternoon — carry a compact umbrella.

**2027-07-18**
- Morning: Morning Yin Yoga or meditation session at Yoga Barn (IDR 150,000). Afterward, visit the Ubud Palace and surrounding streets for a peaceful morning walk before the crowds arrive.
- Afternoon: Day trip to Tegalalang Rice Terraces (approx. 20 mins from Ubud by scooter or taxi). Walk the iconic stepped terraces, take photos, and enjoy the scenic viewpoints. Entry donation approx. IDR 15,000–30,000. Visit a nearby coffee plantation for a Luwak coffee tasting (IDR 50,000–80,000).
- Evening: Return to Ubud and browse the night market near Ubud Traditional Market for local snacks and street food.
- Notes: Hire a scooter (IDR 70,000–100,000/day) or use a Grab/Gojek ride-share app for easy transport. Tegalalang is busiest mid-morning — arrive before 10:00 or after 15:00 for a quieter experience.

**2027-07-19**
- Morning: Take a sunrise trek to Mount Batur (Gunung Batur) — depart from Ubud around 02:00 with a guided group. Summit at sunrise for breathtaking panoramic views over the caldera and Lake Batur. Trek duration approx. 4–5 hours round trip. Cost: IDR 350,000–500,000 including guide.
- Afternoon: Return to Ubud by early afternoon. Rest and recover at the hotel. Optional: soak in a natural hot spring near Mount Batur before heading back (entry IDR 50,000–100,000).
- Evening: Quiet evening in Ubud. Gentle stroll and early dinner.
- Notes: Book the Mount Batur trek through your hotel or a trusted operator at least 1–2 days in advance. Bring warm layers, a headlamp, and sturdy shoes — temperatures at the summit can drop to 10–15°C before dawn.

**2027-07-20**
- Morning: Recovery morning — attend a gentle restorative yoga class at Radiantly Alive (IDR 150,000). Take it easy and enjoy a slow morning in Ubud.
- Afternoon: Visit the Agung Rai Museum of Art (ARMA) — one of Bali's finest art museums showcasing traditional and contemporary Balinese art (entry IDR 80,000). Explore the lush garden grounds and catch a cultural performance if scheduled.
- Evening: Attend a Legong Dance performance at ARMA or Ubud Palace — a classical Balinese dance featuring intricate costumes and storytelling (tickets IDR 100,000–150,000).
- Notes: Day 6 is intentionally slower to recover from the Batur trek. The Mozaic dinner is an optional splurge — substitute with a mid-range warung if preferred to stay on budget.

**2027-07-21**
- Morning: Travel from Ubud to Seminyak / Canggu area (approx. 1.5 hrs by private driver — IDR 200,000–300,000). Check in or drop bags at a day-use arrangement, then head straight to the beach. Enjoy the black-sand beaches of Canggu — Echo Beach or Batu Bolong Beach are ideal for a morning swim.
- Afternoon: Rent a surfboard and take a beginner or intermediate surf lesson at Canggu Surf School (IDR 250,000–400,000 for 2-hour lesson). The warm July waves are excellent for surfing.
- Evening: Sunset at Tanah Lot Temple — one of Bali's most iconic sea temples perched on a rock formation (entry IDR 60,000). Arrive 1 hour before sunset for the best views.
- Notes: Arrange a private driver for the Ubud–Canggu transfer and Tanah Lot visit — this can often be bundled for a flat rate. If staying in Ubud for the full trip, this can be done as a day trip returning in the evening.

**2027-07-22**
- Morning: Early morning beach yoga session on Seminyak Beach — many resorts and instructors offer open-air classes at sunrise (IDR 100,000–150,000). Follow with a refreshing swim in the warm Indian Ocean.
- Afternoon: Explore Seminyak's boutique shopping strip along Jalan Kayu Aya. Browse designer homeware, fashion, and local craft shops. Visit Ku De Ta beach club for a relaxed afternoon by the pool or beach (no entry fee; minimum spend applies).
- Evening: Watch the sunset from Seminyak Beach — one of the best sunset spots in Bali. Enjoy cocktails at a beachside bar.
- Notes: Seminyak is more upscale than Canggu — budget slightly more for dining and activities here. Taxis and ride-shares are readily available.

**2027-07-23**
- Morning: Day trip to Uluwatu — travel approx. 45 mins south by driver. Visit Uluwatu Temple (Pura Luhur Uluwatu) perched dramatically on a 70-metre clifftop above the ocean (entry IDR 50,000). Watch for monkeys and enjoy the stunning coastal views.
- Afternoon: Head down to Padang Padang Beach or Bingin Beach — two of Bali's most beautiful and secluded beaches. Swim, snorkel, or simply relax on the white sand (entry IDR 20,000–30,000).
- Evening: Watch the legendary Kecak Fire Dance at Uluwatu Temple at sunset — widely regarded as the most spectacular setting for this performance (tickets IDR 150,000). Return to Seminyak/Canggu or Ubud by private driver.
- Notes: Hire a private driver for the Uluwatu day trip — IDR 350,000–500,000 for the full day including all stops. Wear a sarong at Uluwatu Temple; they are provided at the entrance.

**2027-07-24**
- Morning: Return to Ubud (if you've been based in the south) or enjoy a morning yoga class at Yoga Barn. Visit the Campuhan Ridge Walk — a scenic 2 km trail through lush green hills just outside Ubud town. Best done early morning before the heat.
- Afternoon: Take a Balinese cooking class — learn to prepare traditional dishes like satay, lawar, and black rice pudding. Classes run approx. 3–4 hours and include a market visit; cost IDR 300,000–500,000 per person.
- Evening: Enjoy the fruits of your cooking class as dinner, or head out to a favourite Ubud restaurant for a celebratory meal.
- Notes: Book the cooking class at least 2 days in advance. Popular options include Paon Bali Cooking Class and Casa Luna Cooking School. The Campuhan Ridge Walk is best before 08:00 to avoid heat and crowds.

**2027-07-25**
- Morning: Visit Besakih Temple — Bali's largest and most sacred Mother Temple located on the slopes of Mount Agung (approx. 1.5 hrs from Ubud). Entry IDR 60,000. Hire a local guide for context and to navigate the complex (IDR 100,000–150,000).
- Afternoon: Stop at the Sidemen Valley on the return journey — one of Bali's most scenic and untouched areas. Walk through terraced rice fields and visit a traditional weaving village where artisans craft ikat textiles by hand.
- Evening: Return to Ubud for a relaxing evening. Book a traditional Javanese or Balinese spa treatment — a 90-minute package at a mid-range spa costs IDR 250,000–400,000.
- Notes: Hire a private driver for the Besakih and Sidemen day trip — IDR 400,000–600,000 for the full day. Dress modestly and bring a sarong for temple visits.

**2027-07-26**
- Morning: Final morning yoga session — attend a special 2-hour workshop at Yoga Barn focusing on pranayama (breathwork) and meditation (IDR 200,000–300,000). A meaningful way to consolidate your yoga practice before departing Bali.
- Afternoon: Explore Ubud's galleries and boutiques one last time. Visit the Neka Art Museum for a comprehensive overview of Balinese painting styles (entry IDR 75,000). Pick up final souvenirs — quality items include silver jewellery, batik fabric, and hand-carved wooden items.
- Evening: Farewell sunset walk along the Campuhan Ridge. Reflect on the journey and enjoy Ubud's peaceful evening atmosphere.
- Notes: Use the afternoon to repack, settle any hotel bills, and confirm your departure transport for Day 13. Leave a review for standout experiences to help future travellers.

**2027-07-27**
- Morning: Final morning in Bali — wake up early for one last sunrise meditation or a peaceful walk in the hotel garden. Pack remaining belongings and check out of Ubud Boutique Stay.
- Afternoon: Arrange a private transfer from Ubud to Ngurah Rai International Airport (approx. 1.5–2 hrs; IDR 200,000–350,000). Allow at least 3 hours before your flight for check-in and security. Browse the airport's duty-free shops for last-minute Balinese coffee, chocolate, or handicrafts.
- Evening: Depart Bali — carry warm memories of rice terraces, temple ceremonies, ocean sunsets, and yoga sunrises.
- Notes: Book your airport transfer the night before to ensure punctuality. Bali's traffic can be unpredictable — always allow extra travel time. Check airline baggage allowances if you've purchased souvenirs. Selamat jalan — safe travels!
