## Day by day

**2027-09-15 (Tuesday)**
- Morning: Arrival at Faro Airport at 13:00 — no morning activities scheduled. Collect luggage, pick up a rental car (highly recommended for Algarve exploration), and drive ~45 minutes west to Praia da Marinha, Lagoa Boutique Stay. Check in, freshen up, and settle into your room.
- Afternoon: Take a gentle first walk down to Praia da Marinha beach (a short walk from the hotel). This stunning cove with golden limestone cliffs and crystal-clear turquoise water is one of Portugal's most iconic beaches. Swim, relax on the sand, and soak in your first Algarve sunset views from the clifftop trail.
- Evening: Head into the nearby village of Carvoeiro (10-minute drive) for a relaxed first dinner. Stroll the charming main square and beachfront promenade before dinner.
- Notes: Rent a car at Faro Airport for the full trip — essential for exploring the Algarve. September is ideal: warm (26–28°C), less crowded than August, and the sea is still warm. Keep Day 1 light to recover from travel.

**2027-09-16 (Wednesday)**
- Morning: After a proper Portuguese breakfast at the hotel, drive 20 minutes west to explore the Benagil Sea Cave — one of the Algarve's most spectacular natural wonders. Book a kayak tour or stand-up paddleboard excursion from Benagil Beach (depart ~9:30 AM before crowds build). Paddle through sea arches and into the famous cathedral cave.
- Afternoon: Continue west along the coast to Praia do Carvalho and Praia de Albandeira — two hidden cove beaches accessible via short cliff walks. Spend the afternoon swimming and snorkeling in calm September waters. Pack a light picnic or stop at a beach kiosk.
- Evening: Return to Carvoeiro village for the evening. Browse the local shops and enjoy sundowners at a clifftop bar before dinner.
- Notes: Book the Benagil kayak tour in advance online — spots fill up quickly even in September. Start the paddle early (before 10 AM) to avoid crowds inside the cave. Wear water shoes and bring reef-safe sunscreen.

**2027-09-17 (Thursday)**
- Morning: Drive 30 minutes east to Silves — the ancient Moorish capital of the Algarve. Visit the impressive red sandstone Castelo de Silves (€3 entry), walk the medieval ramparts, and explore the Cathedral of Silves. The town is quiet and atmospheric in September.
- Afternoon: After exploring Silves, head to the Silves wine region. Visit Quinta dos Vales winery (~15 minutes from Silves) for a guided vineyard tour and wine tasting session. The estate produces excellent Algarve reds and whites. Book ahead for a tasting of 4–6 wines paired with local charcuterie (€15–25 per person).
- Evening: Return to the Lagoa/Carvoeiro area. Have a relaxed dinner in Lagoa town, the heart of Algarve wine country.
- Notes: Silves is best visited in the morning before the heat peaks. The Silves Medieval Fair has already passed (usually August), but the town is beautifully calm in September. Quinta dos Vales requires advance booking for tours.

**2027-09-18 (Friday)**
- Morning: Drive 45 minutes west to Lagos — one of the Algarve's most vibrant historic towns. Explore the old town: walk the ancient city walls, visit the Igreja de Santo António (stunning gilded baroque interior), and the Slave Market Museum (Mercado de Escravos) — the first in the world.
- Afternoon: Head to the Ponta da Piedade headland just south of Lagos — a breathtaking series of golden rock towers, grottos, and sea arches. Walk the clifftop trail (free) for panoramic views, or take a 45-minute boat tour from Lagos Marina to explore the sea caves and grottoes from below (€15–20 per person).
- Evening: Stay in Lagos for dinner and evening drinks. The town has a lively but relaxed September atmosphere with excellent restaurants and bars.
- Notes: Lagos is about 45 minutes from the hotel — park near the marina or old town. The Ponta da Piedade boat tours run until late afternoon; aim for the 3–4 PM slot for golden afternoon light on the rocks.

**2027-09-19 (Saturday)**
- Morning: Drive 1 hour west to Sagres — Portugal's dramatic southwestern tip. Visit the Fortaleza de Sagres (€3 entry), a 15th-century fortress on a windswept promontory where Henry the Navigator planned Portugal's Age of Discovery. Walk the clifftops and look for dolphins in the Atlantic below.
- Afternoon: Drive 10 minutes north to Cabo de São Vicente — the most southwestern point of continental Europe. Stand at the edge of the known world on dramatic 70-metre cliffs. Then head to Praia do Beliche or Praia da Mareta in Sagres for a late afternoon swim in wilder, more rugged Atlantic waters.
- Evening: Return toward Lagos or drive back toward Carvoeiro. Stop in the small town of Vila do Bispo for dinner on the way back.
- Notes: Sagres can be windy even in September — bring a light layer. The drive from Praia da Marinha to Sagres is about 1 hour 10 minutes. This is a full day trip, so an early start is recommended. Cabo de São Vicente sunsets are legendary — time your visit accordingly.

**2027-09-20 (Sunday)**
- Morning: Rest and recharge day. Spend the morning at your home beach — Praia da Marinha. September mornings here are magical with golden light on the limestone cliffs. Take the coastal walking trail along the Percurso dos Sete Vales Suspensos (Seven Hanging Valleys Trail) — a stunning 5.7km clifftop walk east toward Armação de Pêra.
- Afternoon: Finish the Seven Hanging Valleys Trail or turn back midway. Return to the hotel for a pool afternoon. Alternatively, drive 10 minutes to Praia de Vale Centeanes or Praia do Carvalho for a quieter Sunday swim.
- Evening: Drive into Portimão (20 minutes) for a Sunday evening market browse and dinner along the riverside.
- Notes: The Seven Hanging Valleys Trail is one of the most beautiful coastal walks in Europe. Wear sturdy shoes and bring water. The trail is well-marked. Sunday in September is quiet — perfect for a peaceful clifftop walk with fewer tourists.

**2027-09-21 (Monday)**
- Morning: Drive 25 minutes to Portimão and take a boat trip up the Arade River to Silves (approximately 1.5–2 hours each way). The river cruise passes through wetlands, citrus groves, and traditional fishing villages — a wonderful perspective of the Algarve interior. Depart from Portimão Marina.
- Afternoon: Arrive in Silves by boat and spend the afternoon revisiting the castle from a different angle, or explore the local ceramic and cork workshops. Visit the Museu Municipal de Arqueologia. Take the return boat back to Portimão in the late afternoon.
- Evening: Explore Portimão's waterfront and have dinner in the city — a larger, more local town than the tourist centres.
- Notes: Book the Arade River boat trip in advance — operators include Riosul and local marina companies. The round trip takes most of the day. Alternatively, if boat trips aren't running, drive to Monchique mountain village instead for a scenic inland alternative.

**2027-09-22 (Tuesday)**
- Morning: Drive 45 minutes east to Albufeira. Visit the old town (Albufeira Velha) in the morning before the day-trippers arrive — the whitewashed streets, fishermen's beach, and clifftop church are charming in the early light. Explore the local market if it's running.
- Afternoon: Drive 20 minutes east of Albufeira to visit Adega do Cantor winery — famously co-owned by Cliff Richard and producing excellent Algarve wines. Take a guided tour and tasting (book ahead, €15–20 per person). Then visit nearby Quinta da Tôr winery for a second tasting if desired.
- Evening: Return toward the hotel. Stop in the pretty village of Alte — known as the 'prettiest village in the Algarve' — for a brief evening stroll through its flower-draped streets before heading home.
- Notes: Adega do Cantor (Vida Nova wines) requires advance booking for tours and tastings. Albufeira old town is far more pleasant in the morning before the beach crowds arrive. Alte is a 30-minute inland detour but absolutely worth it.

**2027-09-23 (Wednesday)**
- Morning: Drive 1 hour east to Faro — the Algarve's capital and a wonderfully underrated city. Start at the Cidade Velha (old walled city): visit the Sé Cathedral (climb the tower for panoramic views, €3.50), the Renaissance cloister, and the haunting Igreja dos Ossos (Chapel of Bones) inside the Carmo Church.
- Afternoon: Take a ferry from Faro harbour into the Ria Formosa Natural Park — a stunning coastal lagoon system of islands, salt marshes, and tidal flats. Spend 2–3 hours on Ilha Deserta or Ilha de Faro, swimming in calm lagoon waters and walking along pristine beaches backed by dunes.
- Evening: Dinner in Faro city before driving back to the hotel (1 hour).
- Notes: Faro is often overlooked by tourists but is genuinely beautiful. The Ria Formosa ferry runs regularly from Faro harbour — buy tickets at the dock. Estaminé on Ilha Deserta is a bucket-list dining experience but requires a reservation well in advance.

**2027-09-24 (Thursday)**
- Morning: Drive 1.5 hours east to Tavira — widely considered the most beautiful town in the Algarve. Cross the Roman bridge over the Gilão River, visit the Castelo de Tavira for panoramic views, and explore the 37 churches (try Igreja de Santa Maria do Castelo). The town is elegant, uncrowded, and deeply Portuguese.
- Afternoon: Take the cable car or ferry from Tavira to Ilha de Tavira — a long barrier island with one of the Algarve's finest beaches. The water is calm and warm in September, the beach is wide and uncrowded, and the dune-backed landscape is spectacular. Spend 2–3 hours swimming and relaxing.
- Evening: Dinner in Tavira before the drive back — allow 1.5 hours return to the hotel.
- Notes: Tavira is the highlight of the eastern Algarve — don't miss it. The drive from Praia da Marinha is 1.5 hours each way, so plan an early start. September is perfect for Tavira — the summer crowds are gone but the weather and sea are still warm.

**2027-09-25 (Friday)**
- Morning: A dedicated wine day in the Lagoa wine region — right on your doorstep. Visit Adega Cooperativa do Lagoa — the main wine cooperative of the region and one of the oldest in Portugal. Take a cellar tour and taste the distinctive Algarve wines, including the local Negra Mole grape variety (€8–15 for tasting).
- Afternoon: Visit a second local producer: Quinta da Penina or Quinta do Francês near Silves for a more boutique estate experience. Then spend the late afternoon at Praia de Benagil or Praia da Marinha — your home beach — for a final luxurious swim in the September sun.
- Evening: Treat yourself to a special dinner — a slightly elevated mid-range splurge to celebrate the penultimate full day.
- Notes: Bon Bon in Carvoeiro holds a Michelin star and is just minutes from your hotel — booking months in advance is essential. If unavailable, Vila Lisa in Mexilhoeira Grande is an excellent alternative (also Michelin-starred). The Lagoa wine cooperative is open Monday–Friday; confirm hours in advance.

**2027-09-26 (Saturday)**
- Morning: Drive 40 minutes north into the Serra de Monchique — the Algarve's green mountain range. Visit the spa town of Caldas de Monchique, a charming 19th-century thermal village tucked in a wooded valley. Walk the shaded paths, taste the famous mineral water, and browse the local medronho (arbutus berry brandy) shops.
- Afternoon: Continue up to the summit of Fóia (902m) — the highest point in the Algarve — for sweeping views over the entire region, from the coast to the Spanish border on a clear day. Return via Monchique town, visiting the ruined Convento de Nossa Senhora do Desterro and the Friday market stalls (if still running).
- Evening: Return to the coast for a final Saturday evening in Carvoeiro — enjoy the beach bars, watch the sunset, and have a relaxed farewell dinner.
- Notes: Monchique is a wonderful contrast to the coastal Algarve — lush, green, and cool. The drive up to Fóia is on narrow mountain roads — take it slowly. Buy a bottle of medronho brandy as a souvenir. September weekends in Carvoeiro are lively but not overwhelming.

**2027-09-27 (Sunday)**
- Morning: Final morning — take one last slow walk down to Praia da Marinha. Swim, sit on the rocks, and take in the extraordinary limestone cliffs one final time. Pack up and check out of the boutique hotel. Drive to Lagoa town for a final Portuguese breakfast at a local café.
- Afternoon: Drive back toward Faro Airport (45 minutes). If time allows before your flight, stop at the Faro waterfront for a final coffee and stroll along the marina promenade, or browse the Faro market for last-minute local souvenirs: Algarve wines, local honey, almond sweets (Dom Rodrigos), and dried figs.
- Evening: Depart from Faro Airport. Carry memories of golden cliffs, turquoise coves, and glasses of Lagoa wine.
- Notes: Allow at least 1.5 hours before your flight for the drive from Lagoa to Faro and airport check-in. Return the rental car at the airport — allow extra time for the drop-off process. Pack any Algarve wines in checked luggage carefully. September flights are generally on time. Boa viagem — safe travels!
