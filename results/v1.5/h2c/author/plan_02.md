## Day by day

**2027-07-15**
- Morning: Arrival at Ngurah Rai International Airport at 13:00 — no morning activities scheduled. Arrange pre-booked airport transfer to Campuhan Ridge Walk, Ubud Boutique Stay (approx. 1.5–2 hr drive).
- Afternoon: Check in to the boutique stay, freshen up, and take a gentle introductory stroll along the iconic Campuhan Ridge Walk trail (2 km loop) — enjoy sweeping views of the jungle-lined river valley and rice paddies to ease into Bali time.
- Evening: Explore the charming Campuhan and central Ubud strip on foot. Browse local art galleries along Jalan Raya Campuhan before settling in for a relaxed welcome dinner.
- Notes: Keep Day 1 light to recover from the flight. Confirm accommodation check-in time in advance. Exchange currency at the airport or use a reputable ATM in Ubud. Stay hydrated — warm humid weather expected (25°C+).

**2027-07-16**
- Morning: Start with a sunrise yoga class at one of Ubud's renowned studios — Yoga Barn or Radiantly Alive offer morning Hatha or Vinyasa flow sessions (90 min, ~IDR 120,000–150,000). Return to the hotel for breakfast.
- Afternoon: Visit the sacred Pura Gunung Lebah temple at the base of Campuhan Ridge, then hire a driver for a half-day cultural loop: Tegallalang Rice Terraces (30 min north) and a stop at a traditional Balinese silver or wood-carving workshop in Celuk or Mas village.
- Evening: Attend a traditional Kecak Fire Dance performance at Pura Dalem Ubud or Arma Museum (starts ~18:30, ~IDR 100,000–150,000). Dinner in central Ubud afterward.
- Notes: Book yoga class and Kecak performance in advance during July (peak season). Bring a light rain jacket — 30 mm monthly rain means occasional afternoon showers possible.

**2027-07-17**
- Morning: Early morning Campuhan Ridge Walk at sunrise (6:00–7:30 AM) for golden light and cooler temperatures — minimal crowds at this hour. Pack water and wear comfortable shoes.
- Afternoon: Head to Ubud Monkey Forest (Sacred Monkey Sanctuary) — a lush 12-hectare nature reserve with ancient temples and long-tailed macaques (~IDR 80,000 entry). Spend 1.5–2 hrs exploring, then browse Ubud Art Market (Pasar Seni) for batik, sarongs, and handicrafts.
- Evening: Take a traditional Balinese cooking class (evening session available at Paon Bali or Casa Luna Cooking School, ~IDR 350,000–450,000 per person) — learn to prepare 5–6 authentic dishes and enjoy your creation as dinner.
- Notes: Wear closed-toe shoes at Monkey Forest and keep bags zipped — monkeys are playful thieves! Cooking class bookings fill fast in peak season; reserve ahead.

**2027-07-18**
- Morning: Restorative morning: 90-minute Yin Yoga or Meditation session at Yoga Barn (~IDR 130,000). Follow with a traditional Balinese massage at a reputable spa near Campuhan — Uma Spa or Taksu Spa offer 60-min treatments (~IDR 200,000–300,000).
- Afternoon: Day trip to Tirta Empul Holy Water Temple (30 min from Ubud) — witness or participate in the sacred melukat purification ritual. Combine with a visit to Gunung Kawi ancient rock-cut temple shrines nearby.
- Evening: Return to Ubud. Explore the vibrant night atmosphere of Jalan Hanoman — street food stalls, live music bars, and artisan shops.
- Notes: Wear a sarong at Tirta Empul (provided at entrance for a small fee or bring your own). Respect temple etiquette — remove shoes and dress modestly. Bring a change of clothes if you plan to participate in the purification bath.

**2027-07-19**
- Morning: Beach day begins! Early departure from Ubud to Seminyak or Canggu Beach (approx. 1–1.5 hr drive south — hire a private driver, ~IDR 300,000–400,000 round trip). Arrive for a morning surf lesson at Canggu's Batu Bolong Beach (beginner-friendly, ~IDR 200,000–300,000 for 2-hr lesson with board and instructor).
- Afternoon: Relax on the beach, swim, and sunbathe. Rent a sun lounger (~IDR 50,000) and soak up the warm Bali sun. Explore the cool cafés and boutique shops of Canggu's trendy Batu Bolong strip.
- Evening: Watch the legendary Bali sunset from La Brisa or The Lawn beach club in Canggu — one drink minimum (~IDR 80,000–150,000). Head back to Ubud after sunset.
- Notes: July is peak surf season in Bali — waves at Canggu are moderate, ideal for beginners with an instructor. Apply reef-safe sunscreen. Arrange driver the night before.

**2027-07-20**
- Morning: Morning Pranayama (breathwork) and Vinyasa yoga session at Yoga Barn or a rooftop yoga class at your hotel if available (6:30–8:00 AM). Afterward, visit the Ubud Palace (Puri Saren Agung) — free entry, beautiful traditional Balinese royal architecture.
- Afternoon: Explore the Ubud region's artistic heritage: visit ARMA (Agung Rai Museum of Art) — excellent collection of Balinese and Indonesian masters (~IDR 80,000 entry). Stroll through the museum's lush tropical garden café for a refreshment break.
- Evening: Attend a Legong or Barong Dance performance at Ubud Palace (starts 19:30, ~IDR 100,000–150,000) — one of Bali's most captivating traditional dance forms.
- Notes: Check the Ubud Palace performance schedule in advance as different dances are performed on different nights. ARMA also occasionally hosts evening cultural events worth checking.

**2027-07-21**
- Morning: Full day at Nusa Penida island — depart early from Sanur Harbor (1.5 hr drive from Ubud + 45-min fast boat, ~IDR 200,000–250,000 each way). First stop: iconic Kelingking Beach (T-Rex cliff viewpoint) — one of Bali's most photographed spots.
- Afternoon: Continue to Crystal Bay — pristine turquoise waters perfect for snorkeling (equipment rental ~IDR 50,000). Visit Broken Beach (Pasih Uug) and Angel's Billabong natural infinity rock pool. Arrange a local scooter or private driver on the island (~IDR 150,000–200,000 for half day).
- Evening: Return fast boat to Sanur, drive back to Ubud. Relaxed evening — gentle walk along Campuhan Ridge at dusk.
- Notes: Nusa Penida is a full-day commitment — depart Ubud by 6:30 AM. Book fast boat in advance (Maruti Express or Rocky Fast Cruise). Wear sturdy shoes for Kelingking viewpoint trail. Bring cash as ATMs on the island are limited.

**2027-07-22**
- Morning: Recovery morning after Nusa Penida. Sleep in, then enjoy a slow morning with a 60-minute restorative yoga or sound healing session at Yoga Barn (~IDR 130,000). Journal, read, or simply enjoy the hotel's surroundings.
- Afternoon: Explore Ubud's surrounding rice terraces by bicycle — rent a bicycle from town (~IDR 50,000–80,000/day) and cycle through Subak irrigation system fields around Campuhan and Sayan village. Stop at Sayan Terrace for views over the Ayung River gorge.
- Evening: Indulge in a luxurious 90-minute traditional Balinese boreh body scrub and massage at Karsa Spa (set in a stunning jungle ravine along the Ayung River, ~IDR 400,000–600,000) — a well-deserved mid-trip treat.
- Notes: Karsa Spa requires advance booking and is accessed via a steep jungle path — wear comfortable footwear. This is an ideal rest and recharge day at the midpoint of the trip.

**2027-07-23**
- Morning: Early morning visit to Mount Batur (Kintamani region, ~1.5 hr from Ubud) — join a guided sunrise trek (depart 2:00–3:00 AM, ~IDR 350,000–500,000 with guide). Reach the 1,717m summit for a spectacular sunrise over the volcanic crater lake.
- Afternoon: Descend and soak in natural hot springs at the base of Mount Batur (~IDR 150,000 entry) to soothe muscles. Drive back toward Ubud via Kintamani village — stop at a local coffee plantation for a free luwak coffee tasting tour.
- Evening: Return to Ubud. Light evening — gentle stretching, early dinner, and early bedtime to recover from the pre-dawn start.
- Notes: Mount Batur trek requires a licensed guide (mandatory). Bring warm layers — summit temperature can drop to 10–15°C before sunrise. Headlamp, water, and snacks essential. Book guide the day before through your hotel or a reputable trekking agency.

**2027-07-24**
- Morning: Second beach day — head to Balangan Beach or Bingin Beach on the Bukit Peninsula (approx. 1.5–2 hr drive south). These are quieter, cliff-backed beaches with crystal-clear water — ideal for swimming and snorkeling. Arrive early to claim a good spot.
- Afternoon: Explore Uluwatu Temple (Pura Luhur Uluwatu) — dramatically perched on a 70m sea cliff (~IDR 50,000 entry). Walk the cliff-edge path with panoramic Indian Ocean views. Stay for the famous Kecak Fire Dance at sunset (18:00, ~IDR 150,000).
- Evening: Dinner at Jimbaran Bay — choose from the famous seafood warungs right on the beach where tables are set on the sand. Fresh grilled lobster, prawns, and fish with sunset views (~IDR 200,000–400,000 per person). Return to Ubud.
- Notes: Balangan and Bingin beaches involve steep staircase descents — wear appropriate footwear. Keep bags secure at Uluwatu (monkeys present). Jimbaran seafood vendors are plentiful — compare menus and prices before committing.

**2027-07-25**
- Morning: Dedicated yoga immersion morning: attend a 2-hour workshop at Yoga Barn (specialty class — Ashtanga, Aerial, or Chakra Balancing, ~IDR 150,000–200,000). Follow with a 30-minute meditation in the studio's garden.
- Afternoon: Visit Goa Gajah (Elephant Cave) — ancient 9th-century Hindu archaeological site just 4 km from Ubud center (~IDR 50,000 entry). Explore the cave shrine, bathing pools, and jungle terraces. Continue to Yeh Pulu — a nearby carved rock relief site off the beaten path.
- Evening: Ubud Night Market exploration — wander the evening market stalls near Ubud Traditional Market for local street food, grilled satay, martabak (stuffed pancake), and fresh fruit.
- Notes: Goa Gajah requires a sarong (available at entrance). Yeh Pulu is a short walk from Goa Gajah through rice fields — bring good shoes. The night market is liveliest between 18:00–21:00.

**2027-07-26**
- Morning: Final full beach experience — head to Seminyak Beach (1.5 hr drive). Morning swim and beach yoga on the wide sandy shore. Visit the stylish Seminyak Square area for last-minute boutique shopping — local fashion, jewelry, and homeware.
- Afternoon: Explore Petitenget Temple (Pura Petitenget) — a sea temple with excellent sunset potential. Walk north along the beach to Batu Belig for a quieter stretch of sand. Optional: paddleboard rental (~IDR 100,000–150,000/hr).
- Evening: Iconic Seminyak sunset at Ku De Ta or Potato Head Beach Club — enjoy a cocktail as the sun dips below the horizon (drinks from ~IDR 120,000). Farewell dinner in Seminyak before returning to Ubud.
- Notes: Seminyak is busier and more commercial than Ubud — keep an eye on belongings on the beach. Merah Putih is a special-occasion restaurant — book a table in advance. Arrange return transport to Ubud by 21:00–22:00.

**2027-07-27**
- Morning: Final sunrise Campuhan Ridge Walk — a peaceful, reflective last morning stroll through the misty jungle ridge. Take photos, breathe in the Ubud air, and appreciate the journey. Return for a slow hotel breakfast.
- Afternoon: Last-minute souvenir shopping at Ubud Art Market — pick up batik fabrics, wood carvings, silver jewelry, and Balinese paintings. Visit a local healer or energy balancing session if interested (Ubud is famous for traditional healers — arrange through hotel, ~IDR 200,000–350,000). Pack and check out.
- Evening: Depart for airport (allow 2–2.5 hrs from Ubud to Ngurah Rai Airport). Enjoy a final Balinese meal near the airport or at a departure lounge restaurant.
- Notes: Check flight departure time and arrange airport transfer through hotel (book the night before). Allow extra time for Bali traffic — July is peak season and roads can be congested. Declare any wooden or food items at customs. Carry a small bag with essentials accessible during the drive.
