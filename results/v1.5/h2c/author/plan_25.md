## Day by day

**2027-04-15 (Thursday)**
- Morning: Arrival & Transfer — Flight LH lands at 13:00, so morning is in-transit. Clear customs, collect luggage, and take the Haruka Express or JR Shinkansen from the airport to Kyoto Station (approx. 75 min from Kansai Airport or 2.5 hrs from Narita). Check in to your Boutique Stay in Maruyama Park, Gion district.
- Afternoon: Gentle Gion Neighborhood Walk — After settling in, take a leisurely stroll through the atmospheric Gion district. Explore Hanamikoji Street with its preserved machiya townhouses, peek into Shirakawa Canal lined with willow trees and early cherry blossoms, and get your bearings around the neighborhood. Visit Yasaka Shrine at the edge of Maruyama Park for a calm first cultural encounter.
- Evening: Cherry Blossom Viewing in Maruyama Park (Night Hanami) — Head to Maruyama Park just steps from your hotel to witness the iconic illuminated weeping cherry tree (shidarezakura) glowing beautifully after dark. This is one of Kyoto's most magical springtime experiences and is peak season in mid-April.
- Notes: Purchase an IC card (Suica or ICOCA) at the airport for seamless transport. April 15 is typically peak cherry blossom season in Kyoto — Maruyama Park will be stunning. Book dinner in advance if possible. Jet lag may be a factor — keep Day 1 light.

**2027-04-16 (Friday)**
- Morning: Cherry Blossom Viewing in Maruyama Park (Daytime Hanami) — Begin your first full day with a proper morning hanami picnic in Maruyama Park. Arrive early to secure a spot under the famous weeping cherry tree before crowds build. Explore the surrounding garden paths and smaller shrines within the park.
- Afternoon: Kiyomizudera Temple & Ninenzaka/Sannenzaka Lanes — Walk uphill from Gion to the iconic Kiyomizudera Temple, a UNESCO World Heritage Site with sweeping views over Kyoto and cherry blossom-framed rooftops. Descend through the beautifully preserved stone-paved lanes of Ninenzaka and Sannenzaka, lined with traditional craft shops and tea houses.
- Evening: Gion Geisha District Evening Stroll — Return to Gion at dusk for the best chance of spotting geiko (geisha) and maiko (apprentice geisha) walking to evening appointments along Hanamikoji Street. Visit Yasaka Shrine again for the evening atmosphere and lantern glow.
- Notes: Kiyomizudera charges a ¥500 entrance fee. Wear comfortable walking shoes — the uphill walk is moderate. Nishiki Market ('Kyoto's Kitchen') is great for snack shopping and a cultural experience in itself. Gion geisha spotting is best between 17:30–19:30.

**2027-04-17 (Saturday)**
- Morning: Arashiyama Bamboo Grove and Togetsukyo Bridge — Take Bus 11 or the Randen tram from Gion to Arashiyama (approx. 40 min). Arrive early (by 8:00 AM) to experience the ethereal bamboo grove before tour groups arrive. Walk the towering green tunnels of Sagano Bamboo Forest and enjoy the tranquil atmosphere. Cross the scenic Togetsukyo Bridge over the Oi River with mountain and cherry blossom backdrops.
- Afternoon: Tenryuji Temple and Arashiyama Monkey Park — Explore Tenryuji Temple (UNESCO World Heritage), one of Kyoto's finest Zen gardens perfectly framed by cherry blossoms and mountains. Families can then hike up to Iwatayama Monkey Park for panoramic views of Kyoto and close encounters with Japanese macaques.
- Evening: Return to Gion & Pontocho Alley Dinner — Head back to central Kyoto and explore Pontocho, a narrow atmospheric lantern-lit alley running parallel to the Kamo River. Choose from the many intimate restaurants lining both sides.
- Notes: Arashiyama is extremely popular on weekends — arriving before 8:00 AM is strongly recommended. Monkey Park entrance fee is ¥550 per adult, ¥250 for children. Tenryuji entrance is ¥500 (garden only) or ¥800 (temple + garden). Bring yen cash for smaller vendors in Arashiyama.

**2027-04-18 (Sunday)**
- Morning: Fushimi Inari Taisha Shrine — Take the JR Nara Line from Kyoto Station to Inari Station (5 min). Hike through the iconic thousands of vermilion torii gates winding up Mt. Inari. Go as early as possible (7:00–8:00 AM) for a peaceful, crowd-free experience. The lower trails take 30–45 min; the full mountain hike is 2–3 hours.
- Afternoon: Nishiki Market & Downtown Kyoto Exploration — Return to central Kyoto and spend the afternoon exploring Nishiki Market ('Kyoto's Kitchen') for food tastings — pickled vegetables, fresh mochi, grilled skewers, and Japanese sweets. Browse the nearby Teramachi and Shinkyogoku shopping arcades for souvenirs and local goods.
- Evening: Kamo River Sunset & Pontocho Stroll — Walk along the Kamo River banks at sunset, a beloved local tradition. Watch families and couples picnic along the riverside. Enjoy a relaxed evening exploring Pontocho or the Gion area near your hotel.
- Notes: Fushimi Inari is free to enter and open 24 hours. The full hike to the summit and back is approximately 4 km and moderately strenuous — good for families with older children. Wear comfortable shoes. Sunday markets and shopping areas will be busy — expect crowds in Nishiki.

**2027-04-19 (Monday)**
- Morning: Day Trip to Nara — Take the JR Nara Line from Kyoto Station to Nara (approx. 45 min). Visit Todai-ji Temple, home to Japan's largest bronze Buddha statue (Daibutsu). Walk through Nara Park where over 1,200 free-roaming sacred deer bow for shika senbei (deer crackers) — a magical family experience.
- Afternoon: Kasuga Taisha Shrine & Naramachi — Explore the atmospheric Kasuga Taisha Grand Shrine, one of Japan's most important Shinto shrines, with hundreds of bronze and stone lanterns lining its forested paths. Stroll through Naramachi, Nara's historic merchant district with preserved machiya townhouses and craft shops.
- Evening: Return to Kyoto & Relaxed Gion Evening — Return to Kyoto by early evening. Enjoy a quiet dinner near your hotel in the Gion district, reflecting on the day's history and culture.
- Notes: Deer crackers (shika senbei) cost ¥200 per bundle — a highlight for children. Todai-ji entrance is ¥600 per adult, ¥300 for children. Kasuga Taisha is free to enter (inner sanctuary ¥500). A JR Pass or individual tickets (approx. ¥720 each way) cover the Kyoto–Nara journey.

**2027-04-20 (Tuesday)**
- Morning: Travel Day: Kyoto to Hiroshima via Shinkansen — Check out of your Kyoto boutique stay and take the Shinkansen (Sakura or Hikari) from Kyoto Station to Hiroshima (approx. 1.5 hours). Check in to a mid-range hotel in central Hiroshima (e.g., Rihga Royal Hotel or ANA Crowne Plaza Hiroshima).
- Afternoon: Hiroshima Peace Memorial Museum and Park — Visit the profoundly moving Hiroshima Peace Memorial Museum and Peace Memorial Park, a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Walk past the iconic A-Bomb Dome (Genbaku Dome), the Children's Peace Monument, and the Flame of Peace. The museum provides a deeply educational and emotional experience for families — allow 2–3 hours.
- Evening: Hiroshima Castle & Shukkeien Garden — Visit Hiroshima Castle (rebuilt 1958), a symbol of the city's remarkable resilience and reconstruction. Nearby Shukkeien Garden is a beautiful traditional Japanese garden perfect for a peaceful evening stroll.
- Notes: The Peace Memorial Museum (¥200 per adult, free for children under 18) is emotionally intense — prepare children sensitively and allow time for reflection. Audio guides are available in English. The A-Bomb Dome is a free outdoor monument. Shukkeien Garden entrance is ¥260 per adult.

**2027-04-21 (Wednesday)**
- Morning: Day Trip to Miyajima Island — Take the JR San-yo Line to Miyajimaguchi Station, then a 10-min ferry to Miyajima Island. Visit the iconic Itsukushima Shrine with its famous 'floating' torii gate rising from the sea — one of Japan's most iconic images. Explore the shrine complex and walk the shoreline.
- Afternoon: Mt. Misen Hike or Ropeway — Take the Miyajima Ropeway up Mt. Misen for panoramic views of the Seto Inland Sea and surrounding islands. Alternatively, hike the nature trails through ancient cedar forests. Watch the friendly wild deer roaming freely around the island.
- Evening: Return to Hiroshima & Evening at Peace Boulevard — Return to Hiroshima by late afternoon. Take an evening walk along the wide, tree-lined Heiwa Odori (Peace Boulevard) and browse the Hondori covered shopping arcade for souvenirs including Hiroshima's famous momiji manju (maple leaf cakes).
- Notes: The Miyajima ferry is covered by JR Pass. Itsukushima Shrine entrance is ¥300 per adult. The torii gate is best photographed at high tide (check tide tables in advance). Ropeway round trip is ¥2,000 per adult, ¥1,000 for children. The island deer are friendly but may steal food — keep bags zipped.

**2027-04-22 (Thursday)**
- Morning: Travel Day: Hiroshima to Tokyo via Shinkansen — Take the Nozomi or Hikari Shinkansen from Hiroshima to Tokyo (approx. 4 hours). Enjoy the scenic journey past Mt. Fuji on clear days (right side of the train heading east). Check in to a mid-range Tokyo hotel (e.g., Shinjuku, Asakusa, or Shibuya area).
- Afternoon: Senso-ji Temple and Nakamise Shopping Street — Head to Asakusa to explore Tokyo's oldest and most iconic Buddhist temple, Senso-ji, founded in 628 AD. Walk through the dramatic Kaminarimon (Thunder Gate) and the vibrant Nakamise Shopping Street lined with traditional craft stalls, fans, pottery, and Japanese sweets.
- Evening: Sumida River Walk & Tokyo Skytree — Stroll along the Sumida River at dusk and admire the illuminated Tokyo Skytree (the world's tallest tower at 634 m). Consider going up to the observation deck for a spectacular night view of Tokyo's sprawling skyline — an unforgettable family experience.
- Notes: Senso-ji is free to enter and open 24 hours, though the inner hall has specific hours. Nakamise Shopping Street is ideal for souvenir shopping. Tokyo Skytree observation deck tickets: ¥2,100 (lower deck) or ¥3,100 (upper deck) per adult — book online in advance to avoid queues. Sit on the right side of the Shinkansen (seats A/B) for Mt. Fuji views.

**2027-04-23 (Friday)**
- Morning: Tsukiji Outer Market Food Tour — Start the day early (by 8:00 AM) at the legendary Tsukiji Outer Market for a self-guided food tour. Sample fresh sushi, tamagoyaki (rolled omelette), fresh seafood skewers, matcha drinks, and Japanese pickles from the bustling vendor stalls. A must-do Tokyo culinary experience.
- Afternoon: Hamarikyu Gardens & TeamLab Planets (Toyosu) — Walk to the beautiful Hamarikyu Gardens, a traditional Edo-period garden with a charming teahouse on a tidal pond. Then head to Toyosu for TeamLab Planets, an immersive digital art museum with stunning interactive installations — a spectacular family experience combining art, technology, and nature.
- Evening: Shibuya Crossing & Shinjuku Evening — Experience the world-famous Shibuya Scramble Crossing at peak evening rush hour — a true Tokyo spectacle. Browse the neon-lit streets of Shinjuku's entertainment district and explore the Golden Gai alley for a unique atmospheric Tokyo evening.
- Notes: Tsukiji Outer Market is best before 10:00 AM when stalls are freshest and least crowded. Hamarikyu Gardens entrance is ¥300 per adult. TeamLab Planets tickets are ¥3,200 per adult, ¥1,000 for children (ages 4–12) — book well in advance online as it sells out. Wear comfortable shoes for a full walking day.

**2027-04-24 (Saturday)**
- Morning: Meiji Jingu Shrine & Yoyogi Park — Begin your final full day at the serene Meiji Jingu Shrine, Tokyo's most important Shinto shrine set within 70 hectares of forested parkland — a peaceful, spiritual contrast to the city's energy. Walk the forested gravel paths and pass through the massive wooden torii gates. Stroll into adjacent Yoyogi Park, popular with families and locals on weekends.
- Afternoon: Harajuku Takeshita Street & Omotesando — Explore the colorful, quirky Takeshita Street in Harajuku for unique fashion, crepes, and Japanese pop culture — a fun family experience. Then walk along the elegant, tree-lined Omotesando Avenue for upscale shopping, architecture, and a final browse of Japanese design.
- Evening: Farewell Dinner & Packing — Enjoy a special farewell dinner in Tokyo, celebrating 10 wonderful days in Japan. Return to your hotel to pack and prepare for departure.
- Notes: Meiji Jingu is free to enter (inner garden ¥500). The shrine is especially atmospheric in the early morning. Harajuku Takeshita Street is very crowded on weekends — arrive before noon. Confirm your departure flight time and arrange airport transfer in advance — Narita Express (N'EX) or Limousine Bus from Shinjuku are reliable options. Allow 3+ hours before international departure.
