## Day by day

**2027-10-15**
- Morning: Arrival at Tribhuvan International Airport, Kathmandu at 13:00 — no morning activities scheduled
- Afternoon: Clear immigration and customs, collect luggage, and transfer to your hotel in Pokhara via private taxi or tourist bus (approximately 6–7 hours by bus or 25-minute flight). If flying to Pokhara, book an afternoon domestic flight. Settle into your accommodation and freshen up.
- Evening: Take a gentle stroll along Pokhara's Lakeside (Phewa Lake) promenade. Enjoy the calm atmosphere, browse local shops, and watch the sunset reflect off the lake with Annapurna peaks in the background.
- Notes: Exchange currency at the airport or in Pokhara. Keep NPR cash handy as ATMs can be limited on the trail. Purchase a TIMS card and Annapurna Conservation Area Permit (ACAP) in Pokhara before trekking begins.

**2027-10-16**
- Morning: Early morning visit to the World Peace Pagoda (Shanti Stupa) — hike up or take a boat across Phewa Lake and walk up for panoramic views of Pokhara, the lake, and the Annapurna range at sunrise. A perfect acclimatization walk.
- Afternoon: Visit the Annapurna Conservation Area Project (ACAP) office to obtain your ACAP permit and TIMS card if not done yet. Explore the International Mountain Museum in Pokhara to learn about Himalayan geography, flora, fauna, and mountaineering history.
- Evening: Attend a traditional Nepali cultural show at one of Pokhara's local cultural centers, or enjoy a relaxed evening at Lakeside. Pack your trekking gear and prepare for the trail starting tomorrow.
- Notes: Confirm your ACAP permit (NPR 3,000 per person) and TIMS card (NPR 2,000 for individual trekkers). Arrange transport to Nayapul or Phedi for the next morning. Stock up on snacks, water purification tablets, and any trekking supplies.

**2027-10-17**
- Morning: Early morning drive from Pokhara to Nayapul (1.5–2 hours by jeep or local bus, approximately NPR 200–500). Begin trekking from Nayapul toward Tikhedhunga (1,540m), passing through terraced fields, small villages, and rhododendron forests. Trek duration: approximately 3–4 hours.
- Afternoon: Continue trekking from Tikhedhunga to Ulleri (2,073m), famous for its long stone staircase of over 3,000 steps through dense forest. Arrive at Ulleri and check into a teahouse. Rest and explore the village.
- Evening: Relax on the teahouse terrace with views of the surrounding hills. Interact with fellow trekkers and local teahouse owners. Enjoy the cool mountain air.
- Notes: Today's trek covers approximately 11 km with significant elevation gain. Pace yourself on the Ulleri stone steps — they are steep but manageable. Stay hydrated. October weather typically offers clear skies and cool temperatures — ideal trekking conditions.

**2027-10-18**
- Morning: Rise early and trek from Ulleri to Ghorepani (2,874m) through beautiful rhododendron and oak forests. The trail is well-marked and moderately steep. Trek duration: approximately 3–4 hours. Arrive at Ghorepani and check into your teahouse.
- Afternoon: Rest and acclimatize at Ghorepani. Take a short exploratory walk around the village. Visit the local viewpoint for early afternoon views of Dhaulagiri and the Annapurna range. Set an alarm for a very early morning for Poon Hill the next day.
- Evening: Warm up by the teahouse fire with fellow trekkers. Share stories from the trail. The evenings in Ghorepani can be quite cold in October — layer up.
- Notes: Ghorepani is one of the most popular stops on the Annapurna Circuit. Book your teahouse in advance during October (peak season). Temperatures can drop to near freezing at night — bring a warm sleeping bag liner.

**2027-10-19**
- Morning: Wake at 4:30 AM and hike 45 minutes to Poon Hill (3,210m) to witness one of Nepal's most iconic sunrises over the Annapurna and Dhaulagiri ranges. The panoramic view includes Annapurna South, Hiunchuli, Machhapuchhre (Fishtail), and Dhaulagiri. Return to Ghorepani for breakfast.
- Afternoon: After breakfast, trek from Ghorepani to Tadapani (2,630m) through dense rhododendron forest — stunning in October with clear skies. The trail offers continuous mountain views. Trek duration: approximately 4–5 hours.
- Evening: Arrive in Tadapani and settle into your teahouse. Enjoy the quieter atmosphere compared to Ghorepani. Watch the alpenglow on the peaks at dusk.
- Notes: Poon Hill entry fee: NPR 100. Bring a headlamp, warm layers, and your camera for sunrise. The Ghorepani–Tadapani trail passes through one of the finest rhododendron forests in Nepal — in October, the forest is lush green.

**2027-10-20**
- Morning: Trek from Tadapani to Chhomrong (2,170m), one of the gateway villages to the Annapurna Sanctuary. The trail descends steeply through forests before crossing the Modi Khola river and climbing up to Chhomrong. Trek duration: approximately 4–5 hours.
- Afternoon: Arrive in Chhomrong and check into your teahouse. Explore this charming Gurung village with its stone-paved lanes, terraced fields, and spectacular views of Annapurna South and Hiunchuli directly ahead.
- Evening: Visit the Chhomrong viewpoint for golden hour mountain views. Chat with local Gurung families and learn about their culture and traditions. Prepare mentally for the deeper sanctuary trek ahead.
- Notes: Chhomrong is the last village with reliable ATM access before the sanctuary. Withdraw sufficient cash here. The trail into the sanctuary beyond Chhomrong has no ATMs. Stock up on snacks and any personal supplies.

**2027-10-21**
- Morning: Trek from Chhomrong down to Sinuwa (2,360m) via a steep descent to the Chhomrong Khola and then a steep climb back up. Continue through bamboo and rhododendron forest to Bamboo (2,310m). Trek duration: approximately 3–4 hours.
- Afternoon: Continue trekking from Bamboo to Dovan (2,600m) through increasingly dramatic gorge scenery with the Modi Khola river rushing below. The trail enters the Annapurna Sanctuary proper, with towering walls of rock and forest on all sides.
- Evening: Arrive at Dovan teahouse and rest. The isolation and raw natural beauty of this section is extraordinary. Listen to the river and observe the night sky — minimal light pollution makes for excellent stargazing.
- Notes: The trail from Chhomrong into the sanctuary involves significant descent and re-ascent. Trekking poles are highly recommended. Watch for leeches in shaded forest sections — tuck trousers into socks. The sanctuary is a restricted zone — keep your ACAP permit accessible for checks.

**2027-10-22**
- Morning: Trek from Dovan to Machhapuchhre Base Camp (MBC, 3,700m) via Himalaya Hotel (2,920m) and Hinku Cave (3,170m). The landscape transforms dramatically as the valley opens up and the towering peaks of the Annapurna massif come into full view. Trek duration: approximately 5–6 hours.
- Afternoon: Arrive at Machhapuchhre Base Camp and check into your teahouse. Take time to acclimatize and absorb the awe-inspiring 360-degree mountain panorama. The sacred Machhapuchhre (Fishtail Peak, 6,993m) towers directly above.
- Evening: Watch the sunset paint the surrounding peaks in shades of gold, orange, and pink. The silence and grandeur of the high Himalaya at this elevation is deeply moving. Retire early as tomorrow's highlight requires an early start.
- Notes: Altitude at MBC is 3,700m — watch for signs of altitude sickness (headache, nausea, dizziness). Drink plenty of water, avoid alcohol, and ascend slowly. Diamox (acetazolamide) can be taken as a precaution — consult your doctor before the trip. Temperatures drop sharply at night.

**2027-10-23**
- Morning: Wake at 4:30 AM and trek from MBC to Annapurna Base Camp (ABC, 4,130m) in the dark with headlamps to arrive at sunrise. The final approach through the glacial moraine reveals the full amphitheater of the Annapurna Sanctuary — Annapurna I (8,091m), Annapurna South, Gangapurna, and Machhapuchhre surrounding you on all sides. A truly life-changing experience.
- Afternoon: Spend the morning and early afternoon at ABC — explore the base camp area, take photographs, and soak in the extraordinary environment. After lunch, begin the descent back to MBC or continue to Dovan depending on energy levels.
- Evening: Overnight at MBC or Dovan. Celebrate reaching ABC with fellow trekkers over a warm meal. Reflect on the achievement.
- Notes: ABC at 4,130m is the highest point of this itinerary. Spend at least 2–3 hours here to fully appreciate the surroundings. The descent from ABC to MBC takes approximately 1.5 hours. If feeling strong, continue to Dovan to make the return journey more manageable. October typically offers excellent visibility at ABC.

**2027-10-24**
- Morning: Begin the descent from Dovan (or MBC) back through the sanctuary. Trek to Bamboo or Sinuwa, retracing the trail through bamboo and rhododendron forest. The descent is significantly easier on the lungs but harder on the knees — use trekking poles.
- Afternoon: Continue descending to Chhomrong. Enjoy the return views of the Annapurna range from a different perspective. Check back into a teahouse in Chhomrong.
- Evening: Celebrate the successful Annapurna Sanctuary trek with a well-deserved hot shower (available in Chhomrong teahouses) and a hearty meal. Rest tired legs.
- Notes: The descent from the sanctuary involves the same steep sections encountered on the way up — take it slowly to protect your knees. Trekking poles and proper footwear are essential. You've now completed the core Annapurna Sanctuary trek — a major accomplishment.

**2027-10-25**
- Morning: Trek from Chhomrong back toward the lower trails. Descend to Jhinu Danda (1,780m), a small village famous for its natural hot springs on the banks of the Modi Khola river. Trek duration: approximately 2–3 hours.
- Afternoon: Spend the afternoon soaking in the Jhinu Danda natural hot springs — the perfect remedy for sore trekking muscles. The hot springs are set in a beautiful riverside location. Continue trekking to New Bridge or Chomrong area for overnight.
- Evening: Relax in the peaceful riverside setting. Enjoy the sounds of the Modi Khola and the relief of lower altitude. Prepare for the final trekking days ahead.
- Notes: Jhinu Danda hot springs entry fee: approximately NPR 200–300 per person. Bring a towel and change of clothes. The springs are a beloved highlight for returning trekkers. October temperatures make the hot springs especially enjoyable after the cold nights at altitude.

**2027-10-26**
- Morning: Trek from the Chomrong/New Bridge area back to Nayapul via a lower trail route, passing through terraced farmland, small Gurung villages, and riverside paths. Trek duration: approximately 4–5 hours. Arrive in Nayapul and arrange jeep or bus transport back to Pokhara.
- Afternoon: Arrive back in Pokhara by early afternoon. Check back into your Pokhara hotel or guesthouse. Enjoy a long hot shower, laundry service, and the comforts of a proper hotel after days on the trail.
- Evening: Celebrate the completion of your Annapurna trek with a special dinner at one of Pokhara's better restaurants. Enjoy a lakeside walk under the stars with the Annapurna range silhouetted in the distance.
- Notes: Jeep from Nayapul to Pokhara costs approximately NPR 300–500 per seat in a shared vehicle. Book accommodation in Pokhara in advance as October is peak season. Consider a mid-range hotel with a mountain view room for the final Pokhara nights.

**2027-10-27**
- Morning: Well-deserved rest morning in Pokhara. Sleep in and enjoy a leisurely breakfast. Take a gentle boat ride on Phewa Lake — rent a rowing boat (NPR 400–600 per hour) and paddle to the Tal Barahi Temple on the lake's island. The reflection of Machhapuchhre in the lake is iconic.
- Afternoon: Visit the Gupteshwor Mahadev Cave near Davis Falls — an impressive cave system with a sacred Shiva shrine inside. Explore the colorful Lakeside bazaar and pick up trekking souvenirs, Nepali handicrafts, thangka paintings, or pashmina scarves as gifts.
- Evening: Sunset paragliding over Pokhara and Phewa Lake from Sarangkot — one of the world's best paragliding destinations. Tandem flights with certified operators cost approximately USD 80–100. Book in advance through a reputable operator.
- Notes: Paragliding is weather-dependent — October generally offers excellent conditions. Confirm your flight with the operator the evening before. Davis Falls and Gupteshwor Cave entry: approximately NPR 30–50 each. This is a wonderful recovery and exploration day after the intense trek.

**2027-10-28**
- Morning: Final morning in Nepal. Wake early for one last sunrise view of the Annapurna range from your hotel or Lakeside. Pack your bags and check out. If time allows, visit the Bindabasini Temple — the main Hindu temple in Pokhara — for a cultural farewell.
- Afternoon: Transfer to Pokhara Airport for a domestic flight to Kathmandu (25 minutes, approximately USD 80–120 one way). Upon arrival in Kathmandu, depending on your international departure time, visit Thamel for last-minute shopping or the famous Boudhanath Stupa or Pashupatinath Temple if time permits.
- Evening: Transfer to Tribhuvan International Airport for your international departure. Reflect on 14 extraordinary days in the Annapurna region — from Pokhara's lakeside to the high Himalayan sanctuary at 4,130m.
- Notes: Confirm your domestic flight from Pokhara to Kathmandu well in advance — October is peak season and flights fill quickly. Allow at least 3 hours before your international departure for check-in and security at Kathmandu. Declare any cultural artifacts or large purchases at customs. Nepal allows export of items under 100 years old with a receipt.
