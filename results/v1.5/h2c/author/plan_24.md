## Day by day

**2027-10-15**
- Morning: Arrival at airport at 13:00 — skip morning activities. Clear immigration, collect luggage, and arrange transfer to Phi Phi Islands via ferry from Krabi or Phuket pier.
- Afternoon: Board the afternoon ferry to Phi Phi Don Island (approximately 1.5–2 hours). Enjoy scenic views of limestone karsts and turquoise Andaman Sea en route. Check in to your Boutique Stay on Phi Phi Islands and freshen up.
- Evening: Take a leisurely stroll along Tonsai Village's main walking street. Explore the local night market and get oriented with the island layout. Watch the sunset from the Phi Phi Viewpoint if energy allows (20-minute hike from the village).
- Notes: Book ferry tickets in advance during October as it is still shoulder/high season. Keep important documents and valuables in your carry-on during the boat transfer. October may bring occasional rain showers — pack a light rain jacket.

**2027-10-16**
- Morning: Wake up early for a sunrise walk to Phi Phi Viewpoint (Koh Phi Phi Don). The 20-minute hike rewards you with a stunning panoramic view of both bays. Return for a relaxed breakfast near the hotel.
- Afternoon: Join a half-day snorkeling longtail boat tour around Phi Phi Leh, visiting Maya Bay (The Beach), Pileh Lagoon, and Viking Cave. Swim in crystal-clear waters and observe vibrant coral reefs and tropical fish.
- Evening: Return to Tonsai Village and explore the beachfront bars along Loh Dalum Bay. Enjoy fire show performances that typically start around 9 PM at the beach bars.
- Notes: Maya Bay has restricted visitor hours and quotas to protect the ecosystem — confirm timings with your tour operator. Bring reef-safe sunscreen, a hat, and plenty of water. Snorkeling gear is usually provided on tours.

**2027-10-17**
- Morning: Rent a kayak or join a guided kayaking tour around the eastern coastline of Phi Phi Don, paddling through hidden coves and shallow turquoise lagoons. Explore Monkey Beach (Ao Ling) by boat or kayak.
- Afternoon: Head to Long Beach (Hat Yao) on the southern tip of Phi Phi Don via a short longtail boat ride. Relax on the quieter, less crowded beach, swim, and sunbathe. Enjoy the calmer waters ideal for swimming.
- Evening: Return to Tonsai for a Thai cooking class or a sunset cocktail cruise around the island (many operators offer 2-hour sunset cruises departing around 5 PM).
- Notes: Longtail boats to Long Beach cost approximately 100–150 THB per person. Monkeys at Monkey Beach can be aggressive — do not feed them and keep bags zipped. Sunset cruises typically cost 600–900 THB per person.

**2027-10-18**
- Morning: Take an early morning ferry from Phi Phi Don to Krabi Town (approximately 1.5 hours). Check in to a mid-range guesthouse in Krabi Town or head directly to Ao Nang Beach area.
- Afternoon: Explore the iconic Railay Beach via a 15-minute longtail boat from Ao Nang (accessible only by boat due to limestone cliffs). Visit Phra Nang Cave Beach and the famous Princess Cave shrine. Swim and explore the dramatic karst scenery.
- Evening: Return to Ao Nang by longtail and explore the Ao Nang Walking Street for street food, souvenirs, and local nightlife.
- Notes: Longtail boats to Railay Beach from Ao Nang cost approximately 100 THB per person each way. Railay can get crowded in the afternoon — aim to arrive before noon. Store your luggage at your accommodation while day-tripping.

**2027-10-19**
- Morning: Join a 4-Island Tour departing from Krabi or Ao Nang — visiting Koh Mook, Koh Kradan, Koh Libong, and Koh Ngai. Snorkel at Emerald Cave (Tham Morakot) on Koh Mook, swimming through a dark sea tunnel to a hidden beach inside.
- Afternoon: Continue the island-hopping tour with beach time at Koh Kradan, known for its crystal-clear waters and vibrant coral gardens. Snorkel among colorful reef fish and soft corals.
- Evening: Return to Krabi/Ao Nang by late afternoon. Relax and enjoy a Thai massage on the beach strip (60-minute traditional massage: 300–400 THB).
- Notes: 4-Island Tours typically cost 800–1,200 THB per person including lunch, snorkeling gear, and longtail/speedboat transfers. Book the day before at any tour shop in Ao Nang. October weather may cause occasional swells — check sea conditions.

**2027-10-20**
- Morning: Visit Tiger Cave Temple (Wat Tham Suea) in Krabi Town — climb 1,237 steps to the hilltop for panoramic views of Krabi's landscape. Start early to avoid midday heat. The temple complex at the base is also worth exploring.
- Afternoon: Explore Krabi Town's local market and the riverside promenade. Visit the quirky Krabi Town Walking Street area and browse local handicrafts, fresh produce, and street snacks.
- Evening: Take a boat trip along the mangrove-lined Krabi River for a sunset cruise, spotting wildlife including macaques, sea eagles, and monitor lizards in their natural habitat.
- Notes: Tiger Cave Temple climb is strenuous — wear comfortable shoes and bring plenty of water. Modest dress is required at the temple (cover shoulders and knees). The sunset mangrove cruise typically costs 400–600 THB per person.

**2027-10-21**
- Morning: Travel from Krabi to Koh Lanta by ferry or minivan (approximately 2 hours). Check in to a mid-range bungalow resort on Long Beach (Hat Khlong Dao) or Klong Nin Beach.
- Afternoon: Rent a scooter or bicycle and explore Koh Lanta's scenic coastal road. Visit the charming Old Town (Ban Ko Lanta) on the eastern coast — a historic fishing village with Sino-Portuguese shophouses, wooden piers, and a relaxed atmosphere.
- Evening: Catch the sunset from the western beaches of Koh Lanta — Long Beach or Sunset Beach are ideal. Enjoy the famous Koh Lanta laid-back evening vibe.
- Notes: Ferry to Koh Lanta from Krabi runs seasonally — confirm schedules in advance. Scooter rental costs approximately 200–300 THB per day. Old Town is best explored on foot and is a great spot for photography.

**2027-10-22**
- Morning: Join a full-day snorkeling trip to the Koh Lanta Marine National Park — visiting Koh Rok Nok and Koh Rok Nai, two uninhabited islands with some of the best coral reefs in the Andaman Sea.
- Afternoon: Continue snorkeling at Koh Ha (Five Islands), known for its spectacular underwater caverns, swim-throughs, and diverse marine life including leopard sharks and sea turtles.
- Evening: Return to Koh Lanta by late afternoon. Unwind with a beachside yoga session (many resorts offer evening classes) or a traditional Thai herbal compress massage.
- Notes: Koh Rok and Koh Ha snorkeling tours cost approximately 1,200–1,800 THB per person including gear, lunch, and park fees. Sea conditions in October can vary — tours may be cancelled in rough weather. Always check with operators the evening before.

**2027-10-23**
- Morning: Explore Mu Ko Lanta National Park on the southern tip of Koh Lanta — hike through jungle trails to a lighthouse with sweeping views of the Andaman Sea. Look out for wildlife including hornbills and monitor lizards.
- Afternoon: Spend a relaxed afternoon at Klong Nin Beach or Bamboo Bay — quieter beaches on the southern end of the island with fewer crowds. Swim, read, and soak in the natural beauty.
- Evening: Head back north to the main beach strip for a street food evening. Visit the Koh Lanta Night Market (held regularly in the high season) for local dishes, fresh seafood, and tropical fruit.
- Notes: National park entrance fee is approximately 200 THB for foreigners. Bring insect repellent for the jungle hike. The lighthouse trail takes about 30–45 minutes one way and offers excellent photo opportunities.

**2027-10-24**
- Morning: Travel from Koh Lanta to Phuket by ferry and minivan combination (approximately 3–4 hours). Check in to a mid-range hotel in Patong, Kata, or Karon Beach area.
- Afternoon: Visit Kata Beach or Karon Beach for a relaxed afternoon swim. These beaches are less hectic than Patong and offer beautiful scenery with golden sand and clear water. Rent a sun lounger and umbrella.
- Evening: Explore Phuket's famous Bangla Road in Patong for the vibrant nightlife atmosphere. Alternatively, visit the lively Karon Night Market for a more local experience.
- Notes: Book ferry and minivan transfers in advance. The journey involves multiple vehicle changes — keep your luggage manageable. Kata and Karon are recommended over Patong for a more relaxed mid-range experience.

**2027-10-25**
- Morning: Take a day trip to Phang Nga Bay — join a tour by longtail boat or sea kayak to explore the dramatic limestone karsts, sea caves, and mangrove channels. Visit the iconic James Bond Island (Khao Phing Kan) and Ko Panyi floating village.
- Afternoon: Continue exploring Phang Nga Bay with a sea kayaking session through hidden hongs (inland lagoons accessible only at low tide). Paddle through cathedral-like cave openings into serene enclosed lagoons.
- Evening: Return to Phuket by late afternoon. Head to Phuket Old Town for an evening stroll along the colorful Sino-Portuguese streets, visiting street art, local cafés, and the Sunday Walking Street market.
- Notes: Phang Nga Bay tours cost approximately 1,200–2,000 THB per person depending on whether you choose speedboat or longtail, and whether sea kayaking is included. Book the day before at any tour desk. The Sunday Walking Street in Old Town is a highlight — don't miss it if visiting on a Sunday.

**2027-10-26**
- Morning: Visit Big Buddha (Phra Phutta Ming Mongkol Akenakkiri) on Nakkerd Hill — a 45-meter white marble statue offering panoramic views over Phuket. Explore the temple grounds and enjoy the peaceful atmosphere.
- Afternoon: Head to Promthep Cape on the southern tip of Phuket for stunning coastal scenery. Then visit Rawai Beach to browse the local seafood market — choose your own fresh seafood and have it cooked at a nearby restaurant.
- Evening: Enjoy a final Phuket evening with a Thai cultural show or Muay Thai boxing match at a local stadium (tickets: 1,000–1,500 THB ringside). Alternatively, enjoy a farewell dinner at a rooftop restaurant.
- Notes: Big Buddha is free to enter but donations are welcome. Dress modestly (sarongs available to borrow). Rawai seafood market is a unique experience — prices are per kilogram and negotiating is acceptable. Check Muay Thai schedules in advance.

**2027-10-27**
- Morning: Depending on your departure flight time, enjoy a final morning swim at your chosen Phuket beach. Visit a local market for last-minute souvenir shopping — Thai silk scarves, elephant pants, local spices, and artisan crafts.
- Afternoon: Check out of your hotel and transfer to Phuket International Airport. Allow at least 2–3 hours before departure for check-in and security. Enjoy a final Thai meal at the airport.
- Evening: Departure from Phuket. Reflect on 13 incredible days of island hopping, street food adventures, and Andaman Sea exploration.
- Notes: Phuket Airport is approximately 30–45 minutes from Patong/Kata depending on traffic. Pre-book your airport transfer for peace of mind (300–500 THB by taxi or 150–200 THB by shared minivan). Declare any food items when passing through customs. Keep receipts for VAT refund claims on eligible purchases.
