## Day by day

**2027-10-15**
- Morning: Arrival at airport at 13:00 — no morning activities scheduled. Rest and prepare for transfer.
- Afternoon: After landing, clear customs and take a shared minivan or taxi to Rassada Pier in Phuket, then board a high-speed ferry to Phi Phi Don Island (approx. 1.5–2 hours). Check into your boutique stay, freshen up, and take a leisurely stroll along the Ton Sai Village beachfront to get oriented.
- Evening: Explore the lively Ton Sai Village night market. Browse local stalls, soak in the island atmosphere, and enjoy your first evening on Phi Phi.
- Notes: Book your ferry ticket in advance during October as it can be busy. Keep your luggage light and manageable for the ferry boarding. October is late rainy season — pack a light rain jacket.

**2027-10-16**
- Morning: Wake up early and hike up to the Phi Phi Viewpoint (30–45 min uphill walk). Enjoy stunning panoramic views of both Ton Sai Bay and Loh Dalum Bay at sunrise. Return to hotel for breakfast.
- Afternoon: Join a half-day snorkeling longtail boat trip around Phi Phi Don — visit Monkey Beach, Shark Point, and the coral reefs near Phi Phi Leh. Spot blacktip reef sharks, colorful fish, and vibrant coral. Book through your hotel or a local operator (approx. 600–900 THB per person).
- Evening: Head to Loh Dalum Bay beach for sunset views. Relax on the beach before dinner. Browse the beachfront bars for a casual drink.
- Notes: Wear reef-safe sunscreen for snorkeling. Bring cash as many boat operators don't accept cards. The viewpoint hike can be slippery after rain — wear good shoes.

**2027-10-17**
- Morning: Full-day island hopping tour to Phi Phi Leh — visit the famous Maya Bay (where 'The Beach' was filmed), Pileh Lagoon (emerald green enclosed lagoon), and Viking Cave. Swim and snorkel in crystal-clear waters.
- Afternoon: Continue the island hopping tour with stops at Bamboo Island for beach relaxation and snorkeling over shallow coral gardens. Enjoy a packed lunch on the boat.
- Evening: Return to Phi Phi Don by late afternoon. Shower and rest. Head out for a relaxed evening walk through Ton Sai Village and enjoy street food from the night stalls.
- Notes: Maya Bay has restricted access with timed entry — ensure your tour operator has the proper permits. Book the full-day tour at least one day in advance. Bring waterproof bags for your belongings.

**2027-10-18**
- Morning: Rent a kayak (approx. 150–200 THB/hour) and explore the coastline of Phi Phi Don independently. Paddle to hidden coves and small beaches inaccessible by foot. Great for photography and peaceful exploration.
- Afternoon: Take a longtail boat to Loh Moo Dee Bay or Laem Thong Beach (northern tip of Phi Phi Don) — quieter, more secluded beaches perfect for swimming and sunbathing. Enjoy the calmer atmosphere away from the tourist crowds.
- Evening: Attend a traditional Thai fire show on Loh Dalum Beach (free to watch from the beach). Many beach bars host these nightly. Enjoy cocktails at a beachfront bar.
- Notes: Laem Thong Beach is a longer longtail ride (approx. 20–30 min) — negotiate the price beforehand (approx. 300–500 THB round trip). Carry drinking water as facilities are limited on northern beaches.

**2027-10-19**
- Morning: Depart Phi Phi Islands by morning ferry to Krabi Town (approx. 1.5 hours). Check into a mid-range guesthouse in Krabi Town. Walk along the scenic Krabi riverside promenade and visit the quirky giant crab statue — a local landmark.
- Afternoon: Visit Wat Kaew Korawaram — a beautiful white Buddhist temple in the center of Krabi Town with golden Buddha statues and ornate architecture. Then explore the Tiger Cave Temple (Wat Tham Sua) — climb 1,237 steps to the summit for breathtaking views over Krabi (allow 1.5–2 hours).
- Evening: Head to the famous Krabi Town Night Market (open Thursday–Sunday) or the weekend Walking Street for incredible street food. If not a weekend, explore the Chao Fa Night Market along the riverside.
- Notes: The Tiger Cave Temple climb is strenuous — wear comfortable shoes and carry water. Modest dress required for temple visits (cover shoulders and knees). Krabi Town is a great base for exploring the mainland.

**2027-10-20**
- Morning: Full-day Four Islands Tour departing from Krabi — visit Koh Lading (snorkeling), Koh Mook (Emerald Cave — swim through a dark tunnel to a hidden inland beach), Koh Ngai (stunning turquoise waters), and Koh Kradan (pristine white sand). Tour departs around 8:30 AM.
- Afternoon: Continue the Four Islands Tour. Enjoy swimming, snorkeling, and relaxing on the beautiful beaches of Koh Ngai and Koh Kradan. Packed lunch is typically included.
- Evening: Return to Krabi Town by late afternoon. Rest and freshen up. Explore the riverside cafés and enjoy a relaxed dinner.
- Notes: Book the Four Islands Tour through your guesthouse or a reputable local operator (approx. 800–1,200 THB per person). The Emerald Cave visit requires swimming through a dark 80-meter tunnel — bring a small waterproof flashlight if possible.

**2027-10-21**
- Morning: Take a longtail boat from Krabi to Railay Beach (15–20 min, approx. 100–150 THB one way) — this stunning beach is only accessible by boat due to surrounding limestone cliffs. Explore Railay West Beach and Railay East Beach.
- Afternoon: Hike to Phra Nang Cave (Princess Cave) — a sacred cave shrine on the beach filled with colorful offerings. Then hike the jungle trail to the Railay Viewpoint for panoramic views. Optional: try rock climbing on the famous limestone cliffs (beginner courses available from approx. 1,000–1,500 THB).
- Evening: Watch the sunset from Railay West Beach — one of the most photogenic sunsets in Thailand. Return to Krabi by longtail boat. Enjoy a relaxed dinner.
- Notes: Railay Beach is cash only — bring enough baht before boarding the longtail. The viewpoint hike involves rope climbing on steep sections — wear sturdy shoes. Longtail boats run until around 6–7 PM.

**2027-10-22**
- Morning: Travel from Krabi to Koh Lanta by ferry or minivan-ferry combo (approx. 2–2.5 hours, 350–500 THB). Check into a mid-range bungalow resort on Long Beach (Hat Khlong Dao) — the most popular and beautiful beach on Koh Lanta. Settle in and enjoy the beach.
- Afternoon: Rent a scooter (approx. 200–300 THB/day) and explore Koh Lanta. Visit the charming Old Town (Ban Ko Lanta) — a stilted fishing village with colorful wooden shophouses, art galleries, and a multicultural Muslim-Buddhist heritage. Browse local craft shops.
- Evening: Ride to Kantiang Bay or Klong Nin Beach for a spectacular west-facing sunset. Watch the sun dip below the Andaman Sea horizon. Return to Long Beach for dinner.
- Notes: Scooter rental is the best way to explore Koh Lanta — the island is 30 km long. Always wear a helmet. October can bring afternoon rain showers — carry a light poncho. Koh Lanta has a more relaxed, less commercialized vibe than Phi Phi.

**2027-10-23**
- Morning: Visit Mu Ko Lanta National Park at the southern tip of the island — explore jungle trails, visit the lighthouse with panoramic views, and spot wildlife including monitor lizards and tropical birds. Entry fee approx. 200 THB.
- Afternoon: Join a snorkeling day trip from Koh Lanta to the pristine Koh Rok islands (approx. 1,200–1,500 THB) — renowned for some of the clearest water and best coral reefs in the Andaman Sea. Alternatively, relax on Bamboo Bay (a quiet, sheltered cove within the national park).
- Evening: Return to Long Beach. Enjoy a beachfront massage (Thai massage approx. 300–400 THB/hour). Watch the sunset with a cocktail from a beach bar.
- Notes: Koh Rok is a protected marine park with limited daily visitors — book the snorkeling trip early. The national park lighthouse trail is manageable but can be muddy after rain. Bring insect repellent for the jungle trails.

**2027-10-24**
- Morning: Take a Thai cooking class on Koh Lanta (approx. 1,000–1,500 THB for a half-day class) — learn to make Southern Thai dishes like massaman curry, green papaya salad, and pad thai. Most classes include a market visit.
- Afternoon: Spend a leisurely afternoon on Klong Nin Beach — a quieter, more laid-back beach on the west coast with a bohemian vibe, beach bars, and excellent swimming. Rent a sunbed and relax.
- Evening: Head back to Long Beach for the evening. Browse the small night market near the beach road for souvenirs and street snacks. Enjoy a final relaxed evening on Koh Lanta.
- Notes: The cooking class is a highlight — book in advance as classes fill up quickly. Time for Lime and Lanta Thai Cookery School are both well-regarded. Pick up local spice pastes and souvenirs at the market during the class visit.

**2027-10-25**
- Morning: Depart Koh Lanta by ferry back to Krabi, then connect to Phuket by minivan or bus (total travel approx. 3–4 hours). Check into a mid-range hotel in Patong Beach or Kata Beach area. Freshen up.
- Afternoon: Explore Kata Beach or Karon Beach — both are beautiful, less hectic alternatives to Patong. Swim in the warm Andaman waters and enjoy the beach. Browse the local shops and beachfront cafés.
- Evening: Head to Patong Beach for the famous Bangla Road night scene — a vibrant pedestrian street lined with bars, live music, street food, and entertainment. Experience the lively atmosphere (can be busy and loud — go with an open mind).
- Notes: Kata Beach is quieter and more family-friendly than Patong — a good choice if you prefer a calmer base. The minivan/ferry combo from Koh Lanta to Phuket can be booked through your hotel or at the pier. Keep valuables secure in busy Patong.

**2027-10-26**
- Morning: Visit Phuket Old Town — explore the beautifully preserved Sino-Portuguese shophouses, colorful street art murals, and heritage buildings. Visit Thalang Road and Soi Rommanee for the best photo opportunities. Browse local antique shops and boutiques.
- Afternoon: Take a speedboat tour to the famous Phang Nga Bay — visit James Bond Island (Khao Phing Kan), paddle a kayak through sea caves and mangrove tunnels, and marvel at the dramatic limestone karsts rising from the emerald water (approx. 1,500–2,000 THB for a full-day tour).
- Evening: Return to Phuket. Enjoy a farewell dinner at a rooftop restaurant in Phuket Old Town or a beachfront restaurant at Kata Beach.
- Notes: Phang Nga Bay tours depart early (around 8 AM) — arrange pickup from your hotel. The sea kayaking through caves is a highlight — some caves require lying flat in the kayak during low tide. Phuket Old Town is best explored on foot in the morning before it gets hot.

**2027-10-27**
- Morning: Final morning — depending on your departure time, enjoy a last swim at your chosen beach or revisit a favorite café in Phuket Old Town. Do any last-minute souvenir shopping at the Phuket Weekend Market (Naka Market) if it's a weekend, or at the local night bazaar.
- Afternoon: Head to Phuket International Airport for your departure. Allow at least 2–3 hours before your flight for the journey and check-in. Reflect on 13 incredible days of beaches, island hopping, and street food adventures across Thailand's Andaman Coast.
- Evening: Departure — safe travels!
- Notes: Pack any liquid souvenirs (fish sauce, coconut oil, curry pastes) in your checked luggage. Keep receipts for VAT refund claims at the airport (minimum purchase 2,000 THB per store). Exchange remaining Thai baht before departure or at the airport. Leave a review for your favorite guesthouses and tour operators to support local businesses!
