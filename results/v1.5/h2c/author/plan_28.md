## Day by day

**2027-08-15 (Sunday)**
- Morning: Arrival at Barcelona Airport at 13:00 — no morning activities scheduled. Transfer to Barcelona Boutique Stay in Eixample district. Check in, freshen up, and get oriented in the neighbourhood.
- Afternoon: Take a leisurely self-guided walk along Passeig de Gràcia to admire the famous 'Block of Discord' — Casa Batlló, Casa Amatller, and Casa Lleó Morera — iconic Modernista facades right on your doorstep in Eixample. Browse the upscale boutiques and soak in the architectural grandeur without needing a ticket.
- Evening: Head to the Gothic Quarter (Barri Gòtic) for a relaxed first evening. Stroll the narrow medieval lanes, visit Plaça Reial, and settle into a welcoming tapas bar for dinner. End the night with a drink at one of the atmospheric bars around Plaça George Orwell.
- Notes: Keep Day 1 light — jet lag and travel fatigue are real. Pick up a T-Casual metro card (10 trips, ~€11.35) at the airport or any metro station to save money on transport all week.

**2027-08-16 (Monday)**
- Morning: Sagrada Família Guided Tour — Book the first morning slot (9:00 or 9:30 AM) to beat the crowds. The guided tour reveals Gaudí's intricate symbolism, the towering naves, and the stunning stained-glass light show. Allow 2–2.5 hours. The basilica is a short metro or walk from Eixample.
- Afternoon: After the Sagrada Família, walk or take the metro to Park Güell. Explore Gaudí's whimsical mosaic terraces, the Hypostyle Room, and sweeping panoramic views over Barcelona. The Monumental Zone requires a timed ticket (book in advance, ~€10). Spend 1.5–2 hours exploring.
- Evening: Palau de la Música Catalana Evening Concert — Attend a live performance at this UNESCO-listed Art Nouveau masterpiece. Check the schedule in advance and book tickets (€25–50 depending on the concert). The ornate stained-glass ceiling and sculpted interior are as much a spectacle as the music itself.
- Notes: Book Sagrada Família and Palau de la Música tickets well in advance — both sell out weeks ahead, especially in August. Sagrada Família with guided tour costs approximately €26–33 per person.

**2027-08-17 (Tuesday)**
- Morning: Barceloneta Beach & Chiringuito Hopping — Head to Barceloneta Beach for a morning swim and sunbathe session before the afternoon heat peaks. The beach is easily reached by metro (Barceloneta stop). Stroll the passeig and explore the beachfront chiringuitos (beach bars) for a refreshing agua de coco or fresh fruit.
- Afternoon: Paella & Tapas Dinner at Passeig Marítim — Enjoy an extended, leisurely Catalan lunch (Spaniards eat lunch late, 2:00–4:00 PM) at one of the seafront restaurants along the Passeig Marítim. Order a traditional paella de mariscos or fideuà to share. After lunch, walk along the boardwalk toward Port Olímpic and relax. Visit the nearby Barceloneta neighbourhood's market or browse local shops.
- Evening: Beachfront Nightlife at Port Olímpic — Experience Barcelona's legendary nightlife at the Port Olímpic marina. The cluster of open-air bars and clubs comes alive after 11:00 PM. Start with a cocktail at one of the terrace bars overlooking the marina, then move to a club if the mood strikes. CDLC and Pacha Barcelona are popular mid-range options.
- Notes: August beach days are hot (30°C+). Bring sunscreen SPF 50+, a hat, and water. Nightlife in Barcelona starts very late — clubs don't fill up until midnight or 1:00 AM. Pace yourself if you plan to stay out late.

**2027-08-18 (Wednesday)**
- Morning: Recovery morning after nightlife — sleep in and enjoy a slow hotel breakfast or brunch. By mid-morning, visit the Recinte Modernista de Sant Pau, a stunning UNESCO-listed Art Nouveau hospital complex designed by Lluís Domènech i Montaner, just a 10-minute walk from the Sagrada Família. Far less crowded than other landmarks, it's a hidden architectural gem (entry ~€16).
- Afternoon: Explore the El Born neighbourhood on foot — visit the Basílica de Santa Maria del Mar (free entry), a stunning Gothic church built by the people of the Ribera neighbourhood. Browse the trendy boutiques, vintage shops, and art galleries along Carrer del Rec and surrounding streets. Stop at the El Born Cultural Centre to see the ruins of the 1714 city beneath a striking iron-and-glass structure.
- Evening: Rooftop bar evening in Eixample — Barcelona's rooftop bar scene is world-class. Visit the rooftop of La Barceloneta Hotel or the terrace at Hotel Majestic on Passeig de Gràcia for sunset cocktails with skyline views (cocktails €12–18). Then head to the Eixample Esquerra (Left Eixample, also known as 'Gayxample') for a vibrant bar-hopping evening along Carrer del Consell de Cent.
- Notes: Recinte Modernista de Sant Pau is often overlooked by tourists — arrive at opening (10:00 AM) for a peaceful experience. Santa Maria del Mar is free but donations are appreciated. Comfortable walking shoes are essential for El Born's cobblestone streets.

**2027-08-19 (Thursday)**
- Morning: Visit Montjuïc — Take the cable car (Telefèric de Montjuïc) or metro/bus up to the hilltop park for panoramic city views. Explore the Castell de Montjuïc (€5 entry), walk through the gardens, and visit the Fundació Joan Miró if contemporary art interests you (€14). The hilltop is cooler than the city below — perfect for a morning outing.
- Afternoon: Return to the city and spend your final afternoon revisiting favourite spots or doing any last-minute shopping. Mercat de Sant Antoni (recently renovated covered market near Eixample) is excellent for local produce, books, and artisan goods. Alternatively, revisit Passeig de Gràcia for souvenir shopping or visit the interior of Casa Batlló or Casa Milà (La Pedrera) if not done earlier (~€25–35 each, book ahead).
- Evening: Farewell dinner at a special restaurant to close out the trip. Disfrutar (if splurging — Michelin-starred, book months ahead) or a more mid-range option like Bodega Sepúlveda in Eixample for excellent Catalan cuisine with natural wines (€35–50 per person). End the night with a final walk along the illuminated Passeig de Gràcia and a nightcap at a rooftop or jazz bar.
- Notes: If your departure flight is early on Day 6, pack the night before and confirm your airport transfer. The Aerobus from Plaça de Catalunya to the airport runs frequently (€6.75 one-way) and is the most convenient mid-range option. Allow at least 2.5 hours before a morning flight.
