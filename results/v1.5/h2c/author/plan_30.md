## Day by day

**2027-06-15 (Monday)**
- Morning: Arrival & Check-In — Flight LH lands at 13:00, so no morning activities. Use the time to collect luggage, arrange Disney's Magical Express or a rideshare to your Boutique Stay in Magic Kingdom/Lake Buena Vista, and get settled into your accommodation.
- Afternoon: Resort Orientation & Pool Time — After checking in, explore the boutique property, pick up your Disney park tickets or MagicBands, download the My Disney Experience app, and pre-book Lightning Lane selections for the week. Cool off at the resort pool to recover from travel fatigue.
- Evening: Disney Springs Dining & Shopping Evening — Head to Disney Springs (free admission, ~10–15 min from most Lake Buena Vista hotels) for a relaxed first evening. Browse the World of Disney store, enjoy live entertainment on the waterfront, and ease into the Disney atmosphere without the intensity of a full park day.
- Notes: Prioritize rest and logistics today. Set Lightning Lane Multi Pass bookings for tomorrow's Magic Kingdom visit as soon as you connect to Wi-Fi. June weather in Orlando is hot and humid (90°F/32°C+) with afternoon thunderstorms common — pack ponchos and stay hydrated.

**2027-06-16 (Tuesday)**
- Morning: Magic Kingdom Park — Arrive at rope drop (park opens ~9:00 AM; arrive by 8:30 AM for early entry if staying on-site). Head immediately to Tomorrowland for Space Mountain, then Fantasyland for Seven Dwarfs Mine Train using Lightning Lane. Ride Peter Pan's Flight, 'it's a small world', and The Haunted Mansion before crowds build.
- Afternoon: Magic Kingdom Continued — After lunch, experience Big Thunder Mountain Railroad, Pirates of the Caribbean, and Jungle Cruise. Use Lightning Lane passes strategically for the afternoon rush. Take a mid-afternoon break at the resort (on-site guests can return and come back) if energy dips — this is the hottest part of the day.
- Evening: Magic Kingdom Nighttime Spectacular — Return to Magic Kingdom for the evening. Secure a viewing spot 45–60 minutes early for the 'Disney Enchantment' or 'Happily Ever After' fireworks show over Cinderella Castle. Ride any remaining attractions with shorter evening wait times before the show.
- Notes: This is the Magic Kingdom Park Full Day Adventure experience. Wear comfortable shoes and bring a refillable water bottle — free ice water is available at any quick-service location. Lightning Lane Multi Pass is highly recommended for Seven Dwarfs Mine Train and Tiana's Bayou Adventure.

**2027-06-17 (Wednesday)**
- Morning: EPCOT World Showcase Food & Culture Tour — Arrive at EPCOT for rope drop (~9:00 AM). Start in Future World/World Discovery to ride Guardians of the Galaxy: Cosmic Rewind and Test Track early before lines grow. Then transition to the World Showcase as it opens at 11:00 AM.
- Afternoon: EPCOT World Showcase Exploration — Stroll through the 11 country pavilions around World Showcase Lagoon. Sample authentic dishes and snacks from Mexico, Japan, France, Morocco, and Italy. Experience Remy's Ratatouille Adventure in the France pavilion (use Lightning Lane) and the Gran Fiesta Tour in Mexico. Explore cultural exhibits and artisan demonstrations.
- Evening: EPCOT Harmonious Nighttime Show — Stay for EPCOT's spectacular nighttime lagoon show featuring massive floating set pieces, fountains, fire, and projections. Find a great viewing spot along the World Showcase promenade. Enjoy a final country pavilion or two before the show begins.
- Notes: This is the EPCOT World Showcase Food & Culture Tour experience. EPCOT is best experienced at a leisurely pace — don't rush the World Showcase. Bring cash or load a Disney gift card for smaller food-stand purchases. The International Food & Wine Festival runs seasonally and may overlap with your June visit.

**2027-06-18 (Thursday)**
- Morning: Disney's Hollywood Studios — Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge — Arrive at rope drop for Hollywood Studios (~9:00 AM, or use Early Theme Park Entry if eligible). Head straight to Galaxy's Edge. Book a reservation for Savi's Workshop (Handbuilt Lightsabers, ~$249.99) or Droid Depot (~$119.99) — these require advance reservations. Ride Star Wars: Rise of the Resistance first thing using Lightning Lane Individual (book at 7:00 AM sharp on the app).
- Afternoon: Hollywood Studios Broader Exploration — After Galaxy's Edge, explore Millennium Falcon: Smugglers Run, then head to Toy Story Land for Slinky Dog Dash and Alien Swirling Saucers. Experience Tower of Terror and Rock 'n' Roller Coaster in Sunset Boulevard. Catch a showing of Indiana Jones Epic Stunt Spectacular.
- Evening: Fantasmic! Nighttime Show — Hollywood Studios' iconic nighttime spectacular featuring Mickey Mouse, water screens, fire, and beloved Disney villains. Arrive 60–90 minutes early for the best seats in the 6,900-seat Hollywood Hills Amphitheater. Enjoy the park atmosphere until closing.
- Notes: This is the Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge Immersive Experience day. Lightsaber building at Savi's Workshop books out weeks in advance — confirm your reservation before arrival. Wear comfortable clothes as June heat in Galaxy's Edge can be intense. The Star Wars theming is fully immersive — cast members stay in character throughout.

**2027-06-19 (Friday)**
- Morning: Disney's Animal Kingdom — Safari & Avatar Experience — Arrive at rope drop (~8:00 AM, one of the earliest-opening parks). Head immediately to Pandora – The World of Avatar for Flight of Passage (the park's most popular ride) using Lightning Lane Individual. Then experience Na'vi River Journey for a calmer but visually stunning boat ride through bioluminescent rainforest.
- Afternoon: Kilimanjaro Safari & Animal Kingdom Exploration — Head to Africa for the Kilimanjaro Safari — aim for early afternoon when animals are often more active in shaded areas. See lions, elephants, giraffes, hippos, and more in naturalistic habitats. Explore Gorilla Falls Exploration Trail on foot. Visit Asia for Expedition Everest and Kali River Rapids (prepare to get wet!).
- Evening: Rivers of Light / Tree of Life Awakenings — As dusk falls, watch the Tree of Life come alive with projected animal spirits — a free, magical experience that occurs every few minutes. Catch the Rivers of Light nighttime show at the amphitheater if running. The park typically closes earlier than others, so plan to depart by 9:00–10:00 PM.
- Notes: This is the Disney's Animal Kingdom Safari & Avatar Experience day. Flight of Passage Lightning Lane Individual sells out by 7:00 AM — book the moment the window opens. Bring a change of clothes or a poncho for Kali River Rapids. Animal Kingdom is a walking-intensive park — comfortable footwear is essential.

**2027-06-20 (Saturday)**
- Morning: Resort Relaxation & Leisure Morning — Take a well-deserved slow morning. Sleep in, enjoy a leisurely breakfast at the resort, use the pool, or explore your boutique hotel's amenities. This recovery day is important mid-trip to recharge energy for the final days. Optionally, visit a Disney resort hotel lobby for free (like the Grand Floridian or Polynesian) to enjoy the theming and atmosphere.
- Afternoon: EPCOT or Magic Kingdom Return Visit / Park Hopping — Use this afternoon for a park hop to catch any missed attractions. Recommended: Return to Magic Kingdom for Tiana's Bayou Adventure, the updated TRON Lightcycle / Run (book Lightning Lane early), or any favorites you want to re-ride. Alternatively, explore EPCOT's Future World area more deeply.
- Evening: Disney Springs Dining & Entertainment — Return to Disney Springs for a festive Saturday evening. Browse unique shops like Uniqlo, Star Wars Galactic Outpost, and the LEGO Store. Enjoy live music at the outdoor stages. This is a great opportunity for souvenir shopping and a relaxed final full-evening out.
- Notes: Saturday is one of the busiest days at Disney parks — consider using this as a lighter day and avoiding the most crowded parks. Disney Springs is always free to enter and a great Saturday evening destination. Use any remaining Lightning Lane credits wisely if park hopping.

**2027-06-21 (Sunday)**
- Morning: Final Park Morning — Magic Kingdom or EPCOT Farewell — Choose your favorite park for one last magical morning. If returning to Magic Kingdom, ride any must-do attractions you missed or want to experience again. Purchase last-minute souvenirs inside the park. Take photos in front of Cinderella Castle for a memorable farewell.
- Afternoon: Resort Check-Out & Final Experiences — Check out of the boutique stay (standard checkout ~11:00 AM; request late checkout if available). Store luggage with the bell desk and use remaining afternoon time for a final Disney Springs visit, resort pool dip, or relaxed stroll. If your flight departs late, Disney's resort luggage storage allows you to enjoy the property until departure.
- Evening: Departure — Head to Orlando International Airport with ample time. Allow at least 2–2.5 hours before your flight for security. Reflect on an amazing week at Walt Disney World!
- Notes: Confirm your flight time and arrange transportation to MCO in advance — rideshare, Disney's Magical Express alternative (Mears Connect), or rental car return. Orlando traffic on Sunday afternoons can be heavy — budget extra travel time. Don't forget to check all hotel rooms for left-behind items before checkout!
