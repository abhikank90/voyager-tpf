## Day by day

**2027-03-15 (Monday)**
- Morning: Arrival at Marrakech Menara Airport at 13:00 — no morning activities scheduled. Transfer to your boutique riad in the Medina near Djemaa el-Fna (approx. 20–30 min by taxi, ~80–100 MAD). Check in, freshen up, and settle into your accommodation.
- Afternoon: Gentle orientation walk around Djemaa el-Fna square and its immediate surroundings. Explore the edges of the souks near Rue des Épices, visit the nearby Koutoubia Mosque gardens (exterior), and get your bearings in the Medina. Pick up a local SIM card from a nearby phone shop if needed.
- Evening: Experience the magic of Djemaa el-Fna at dusk — watch the square transform with snake charmers, storytellers, musicians, and food stalls. Head to a rooftop café overlooking the square (Café de France or Café Argana) for mint tea and the panoramic view. Stroll back for an early dinner and rest after travel.
- Notes: Day 1 is intentionally light given the afternoon arrival. Prioritize rest to recover from travel. Keep your valuables secure in the busy square. Bargain politely if approached by vendors.

**2027-03-16 (Tuesday)**
- Morning: Full morning exploring the historic Medina. Visit the stunning Bahia Palace (entry ~70 MAD) — a 19th-century grand vizier's palace with intricate Moroccan craftsmanship. Then walk to the nearby Mellah (Jewish Quarter) and the old Bab Mellah gate to soak in layers of history.
- Afternoon: Explore the famous souks of Marrakech — navigate through the dyers' souk (Souk Sabbaghin), spice souk, leather souk, and the lamp souk. Visit the Ben Youssef Madrasa (entry ~70 MAD), a breathtaking 14th-century Islamic college with stunning zellige tilework and carved stucco. End at the nearby Marrakech Museum housed in the Dar Menebhi Palace.
- Evening: Return to Djemaa el-Fna for the full evening spectacle. Dine at the open-air food stalls in the center of the square (stalls numbered 1–100) — a quintessential Marrakech experience. Try grilled merguez, brochettes, snail soup, and fresh-squeezed orange juice (budget-friendly: 80–150 MAD for a full meal).
- Notes: Wear comfortable walking shoes — you'll cover 5–8 km today. The Medina can be disorienting; use Google Maps offline or a local guide app. Bargaining in the souks is expected — start at 40–50% of the asking price.

**2027-03-17 (Wednesday)**
- Morning: Visit the Saadian Tombs (entry ~70 MAD) — a royal necropolis dating to the 16th century rediscovered in 1917, featuring ornate marble and cedarwood details. Afterward, explore the atmospheric El Badi Palace ruins (entry ~70 MAD), a once-magnificent 16th-century palace now hauntingly beautiful in its decay, with resident storks nesting on the battlements.
- Afternoon: Take a short taxi ride (~20 min) to the Majorelle Garden and YSL Museum complex. Stroll through the iconic cobalt-blue Majorelle Garden designed by French painter Jacques Majorelle and later restored by Yves Saint Laurent (entry ~150 MAD). Visit the Berber Museum within the garden. The adjacent YSL Museum (entry ~100 MAD) is optional for fashion and design enthusiasts.
- Evening: Head to the Gueliz neighborhood (New Town) for a different atmosphere — browse the art galleries and boutiques along Avenue Mohammed V. Enjoy dinner at a modern Moroccan restaurant in Gueliz for a refined experience.
- Notes: Book Majorelle Garden tickets online in advance — it sells out frequently, especially in spring. March is a beautiful time to visit as the garden is lush. Taxi from Medina to Majorelle is ~30–40 MAD (negotiate before getting in).

**2027-03-18 (Thursday)**
- Morning: Early departure (6:00–7:00 AM) for the Sahara Desert — begin the scenic drive over the High Atlas Mountains via the Tizi n'Tichka pass (2,260m elevation). Stop at the dramatic mountain scenery viewpoints along the way. Arrive in the UNESCO-listed Aït Benhaddou ksar (fortified village) — a stunning earthen citadel used in Game of Thrones, Gladiator, and Lawrence of Arabia. Explore the ksar on foot (entry ~20 MAD).
- Afternoon: Continue driving through the Draa Valley and Dadès Gorge region toward Merzouga and the Erg Chebbi dunes. The landscape shifts dramatically from mountains to desert — red ochre kasbahs, date palm oases, and vast rocky hammada. Arrive in Merzouga village by late afternoon. Check into a mid-range desert camp or guesthouse.
- Evening: Mount a camel at sunset for a 45–60 minute ride into the Erg Chebbi dunes (included in most desert camp packages or ~200–300 MAD separately). Arrive at your Berber desert camp in time for sunset over the dunes — one of Morocco's most iconic experiences. Enjoy a traditional dinner around a campfire with Gnawa music.
- Notes: This is a long driving day (~8–10 hours total including stops). Book a private driver or organized day tour from Marrakech (mid-range: 600–900 MAD per person for a shared tour, or 1,500–2,000 MAD for a private car). Alternatively, join a 3-day organized Marrakech–Sahara tour. Bring layers — the desert is cold at night in March (can drop to 5–10°C).

**2027-03-19 (Friday)**
- Morning: Wake before sunrise (around 6:30 AM) to climb the dunes for a spectacular Sahara sunrise — one of the most memorable moments of any Morocco trip. Watch the shifting colors of the dunes from gold to amber to pink. Return to camp for a traditional Berber breakfast. Optional: sandboarding on the dunes (boards available at camp or nearby rental shops, ~50–80 MAD).
- Afternoon: Explore Merzouga village and the surrounding area. Visit a local Gnawa musician's home for a cultural music session (often arranged by camps, ~50–100 MAD donation). Explore the fossil and mineral shops of Erfoud on the way back. Optional adventure: quad biking across the dunes (1 hour ~250–350 MAD) for adrenaline seekers.
- Evening: Begin the return journey toward Marrakech, stopping overnight in the Dadès Valley or Ouarzazate to break up the drive. Check into a mid-range guesthouse or kasbah hotel in the valley. Enjoy a quiet dinner and stargazing in the clear desert sky.
- Notes: The sunrise experience is the highlight of the Sahara visit — set an alarm and don't miss it. Bring a headlamp for navigating the dunes in the dark. Apply sunscreen generously during the day as the desert sun is intense even in March.

**2027-03-20 (Saturday)**
- Morning: Morning exploration of the Dadès Gorge or Todra Gorge (depending on overnight location) — dramatic limestone canyon walls rising up to 300m. Take a short hike along the gorge floor (easy, 1–2 hours). The Todra Gorge is a popular rock-climbing destination and offers stunning photography opportunities in the morning light.
- Afternoon: Drive back over the Atlas Mountains toward Marrakech. Stop at a traditional Berber argan oil cooperative along the route — learn about the argan oil production process and support local Berber women's cooperatives (free to visit, purchase optional). Arrive back in Marrakech by late afternoon. Return to your riad, shower, and decompress.
- Evening: Relaxed final evening in Marrakech — visit a traditional hammam for a cleansing steam bath and scrub (mid-range hammam: Hammam de la Rose or Les Bains de Marrakech, ~250–400 MAD for a full treatment). Follow with a leisurely dinner at one of Marrakech's finest riads or rooftop restaurants.
- Notes: Book the hammam in advance, especially for Saturday evening as it can be busy. The Rose Valley (Kalaat M'Gouna) is famous for its rose festival in May — in March you'll see the roses beginning to bloom. A mid-range private driver for the full Sahara round trip (Days 4–6) typically costs 2,500–3,500 MAD total.

**2027-03-21 (Sunday)**
- Morning: Final morning in Marrakech — revisit your favorite spot in the Medina or explore anything you missed earlier in the week. Visit the Mellah spice market for last-minute souvenir shopping — saffron, ras el hanout spice blend, argan oil, and rose water make excellent gifts. Wander through the Souk Cherratine (leather souk) for leather goods at better prices than tourist shops.
- Afternoon: Enjoy a leisurely lunch at a rooftop restaurant with views over the Medina. Pack your bags and prepare for departure. Depending on your flight time, arrange a taxi to Marrakech Menara Airport (allow 45–60 minutes for airport procedures). The airport is about 20–30 minutes from the Medina by taxi (~80–100 MAD).
- Evening: Departure from Marrakech — carry the memories of the Sahara dunes, Atlas Mountains, ancient kasbahs, and the vibrant chaos of Djemaa el-Fna with you.
- Notes: Allow extra time at Marrakech Menara Airport — security lines can be slow on Sundays. Keep some MAD for the taxi and any last-minute purchases. Duty-free at the airport offers argan products and Moroccan sweets at fair prices. Tipping culture: 10–15% at restaurants, 10–20 MAD for porters, 20–50 MAD for guides.
