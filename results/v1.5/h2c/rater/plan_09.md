## Day by day

**2027-06-15 (Monday)**
- Morning: Arrival at Rome airport at 13:00 — no morning activities scheduled. Transfer from airport to your Boutique Stay in Vatican City, Borgo district. Check-in, freshen up, and settle into your room. The journey from Fiumicino takes approximately 45–60 minutes by taxi or Leonardo Express + metro.
- Afternoon: After settling in, take a leisurely orientation stroll through the Borgo district. Walk along Via della Conciliazione toward St. Peter's Square for your first iconic view of the Basilica. Admire the exterior of St. Peter's Basilica and the sweeping Bernini colonnade at no cost — a perfect gentle introduction to Rome without overexerting after travel. Browse the charming local streets around Castel Sant'Angelo.
- Evening: Head to the nearby Prati neighborhood (a 10-minute walk from your hotel) for a relaxed first dinner. Explore the vibrant local restaurant scene on Via Cola di Rienzo. After dinner, enjoy a night walk across Ponte Sant'Angelo to see Castel Sant'Angelo beautifully illuminated — one of Rome's most magical evening sights.
- Notes: Keep Day 1 light — jet lag and travel fatigue are real. Pre-book a taxi or arrange an airport transfer in advance. Confirm hotel check-in time to avoid waiting with luggage. June in Rome is warm (25–32°C), so carry water and wear breathable clothing.

**2027-06-16 (Tuesday)**
- Morning: Vatican Museums & Sistine Chapel (pre-booked timed entry — book well in advance, ideally 2–3 months ahead). Start at 9:00 AM to beat the crowds. Spend 3–4 hours exploring the Raphael Rooms, Gallery of Maps, and culminating in Michelangelo's awe-inspiring Sistine Chapel ceiling. This is a world-class art experience steps from your hotel.
- Afternoon: After the museums, visit St. Peter's Basilica (free entry, just a short walk through the Vatican). Climb to the top of the dome for panoramic views over Rome (€8 by stairs, €10 by elevator). Return to your hotel for a brief midday rest during the hottest part of the day.
- Evening: Cross the Tiber for the Trastevere Food & Wine Evening Walk experience. Wander cobblestone streets sampling Roman street food — supplì, artichokes alla giudia, local wines — while soaking in the bohemian atmosphere of one of Rome's most photogenic neighborhoods.
- Notes: Vatican Museums tickets sell out weeks in advance — book online at museivaticani.va immediately upon planning. Dress code enforced: covered shoulders and knees required for both the Vatican Museums and St. Peter's Basilica. Bring a scarf or light layer. Arrive at the Vatican entrance 15 minutes before your timed slot.

**2027-06-17 (Wednesday)**
- Morning: Testaccio Market Culinary Tour — take the tram or a short taxi to Rome's most authentic food market. Join a guided culinary tour starting around 9:30 AM. Sample porchetta sandwiches, aged pecorino, fresh pasta, local cheeses, and seasonal produce. Learn about Roman food culture from local vendors who have supplied the neighborhood for generations.
- Afternoon: After the market tour, explore the Testaccio neighborhood on foot — visit the Non-Catholic Cemetery (final resting place of Keats and Shelley, €3 donation) and the Macro Testaccio contemporary art space. Then head north to the Palatine Hill & Roman Forum Guided Tour (book afternoon slot, ~14:00). Walk through the heart of ancient Rome with an expert art historian guide who brings the ruins to life.
- Evening: Return to the Prati or Borgo area for a quieter evening. Browse local shops along Via Cola di Rienzo, then enjoy an aperitivo hour — a Roman ritual of pre-dinner drinks with complimentary snacks — at a neighborhood bar.
- Notes: The Testaccio Market is best experienced on weekday mornings when locals shop. Wear comfortable walking shoes — cobblestones are everywhere. The Palatine Hill & Forum combo ticket also includes the Colosseum (valid 2 days), so consider whether you'd like to add the Colosseum to a future day. Book the guided tour in advance through a reputable operator.

**2027-06-18 (Thursday)**
- Morning: Borghese Gallery Art Experience — take a taxi or bus to Villa Borghese park (approximately 20 minutes from your hotel). Timed entry is mandatory and strictly enforced (2-hour slots); book at least 3–4 weeks ahead. Discover Bernini's breathtaking baroque sculptures — Apollo and Daphne, The Rape of Proserpina — alongside Caravaggio's dramatic masterpieces in this intimate, world-class setting.
- Afternoon: After the gallery, spend time in the beautiful Villa Borghese gardens — rent a rowboat on the lake, stroll through the park, or visit the Pincian Hill terrace for sweeping views over Rome and Piazza del Popolo below. Descend the Spanish Steps (a short walk from the park) and explore the elegant shopping streets of the Tridente district.
- Evening: Explore the historic center around the Pantheon and Piazza Navona. Visit the Pantheon (timed entry required, €5) in the early evening when crowds thin. Stroll to Piazza Navona to admire Bernini's Fountain of the Four Rivers. Enjoy gelato and people-watching in one of Rome's most beautiful piazzas.
- Notes: Borghese Gallery is one of Rome's most in-demand tickets — do not skip booking in advance at galleriaborghese.it. The gallery limits visitors to 360 per time slot to preserve the experience. June evenings are long and warm — the Pantheon and Navona area are magical at dusk. Bring mosquito repellent for the Villa Borghese gardens.

**2027-06-19 (Friday)**
- Morning: Explore the Jewish Ghetto and Campo de' Fiori neighborhood — a leisurely morning walk through one of Rome's oldest and most atmospheric districts. Visit the Great Synagogue of Rome and its museum (€12), then browse the lively Campo de' Fiori morning market for flowers, spices, and local produce. Cross to the nearby Largo di Torre Argentina — ancient Roman ruins where Julius Caesar was assassinated.
- Afternoon: Take the metro or a short taxi to the Colosseum (your combo ticket from Day 3 covers entry). Explore the iconic amphitheater and imagine gladiatorial spectacles. The adjacent Arch of Constantine is free to view. Alternatively, if you've already used the Palatine/Forum/Colosseum combo, purchase a standalone Colosseum ticket online (€18). Rest at the hotel during peak afternoon heat.
- Evening: Trastevere neighborhood independent evening — return to explore at your own pace without a tour. Discover hidden piazzas, local bars, and authentic Roman nightlife. Visit the stunning Basilica di Santa Maria in Trastevere (free, beautifully lit at night) before dinner.
- Notes: The Jewish Ghetto is particularly atmospheric on Friday mornings before Shabbat. Campo de' Fiori market runs until approximately 13:00. The Colosseum is extremely busy in June — pre-booked tickets with a timed entry are essential to avoid 2–3 hour queues. Consider adding an underground Colosseum tour for an extra fee if interested in archaeology.

**2027-06-20 (Saturday)**
- Morning: Day trip or deeper neighborhood exploration: Head to the charming neighborhood of Monti — Rome's hipster village near the Colosseum. Browse independent boutiques, vintage shops, and artisan studios. Visit the Basilica di Santa Maria Maggiore (free), one of Rome's four major basilicas, with stunning 5th-century mosaics. Explore the nearby Esquilino market for multicultural food stalls.
- Afternoon: Visit the Palazzo Massimo alle Terme — one of Rome's most underrated museums, housing extraordinary ancient Roman frescoes, mosaics, and classical sculptures (€10). Located near Termini station, it's easily accessible. Spend 2 hours exploring before returning toward your hotel. Stop at the stunning Piazza del Popolo and its twin churches if energy allows.
- Evening: Farewell aperitivo evening in Prati — join locals at a neighborhood enoteca (wine bar) for a classic Roman aperitivo with Aperol Spritz or Negroni and cicchetti. Then enjoy a final special dinner in the Prati neighborhood, which has blossomed into one of Rome's best dining destinations.
- Notes: Monti market (Mercato Monti) runs Saturday and Sunday 10:00–20:00 — a great spot for unique souvenirs and local food. Palazzo Massimo is surprisingly uncrowded and deeply rewarding for art lovers. Saturday evenings in Prati are lively — book dinner reservations in advance. This is a good day to purchase any last-minute gifts or artisan goods.

**2027-06-21 (Sunday)**
- Morning: Final morning in Rome — attend Sunday Mass at St. Peter's Basilica (free, deeply moving even for non-religious visitors) or simply visit the Basilica one last time without the weekday crowds. Afterward, take a slow farewell walk through your beloved Borgo district, stopping at a favorite café for a final Roman breakfast ritual.
- Afternoon: Depending on your departure time, visit any missed sites or revisit a favorite spot. The Castel Sant'Angelo museum (€15) is steps from your hotel and offers excellent views from its terrace — perfect for a final panoramic goodbye to Rome. Alternatively, spend the afternoon at a local café writing postcards or journaling. Begin packing and preparing for departure.
- Evening: If your flight departs late or the following day: enjoy a final quiet dinner in Prati or Borgo. If departing this evening, allow 2.5–3 hours to reach the airport from your hotel (1 hour travel + buffer for check-in and security).
- Notes: Check hotel checkout time — most boutique hotels require checkout by 11:00–12:00. Ask about luggage storage if your flight is later in the day. Sunday in Rome means reduced public transport — allow extra travel time to the airport. Leonardo Express trains run every 30 minutes from Termini to Fiumicino (€14 per person). Tip: book a taxi the night before for airport transfer to avoid Sunday morning availability issues.
