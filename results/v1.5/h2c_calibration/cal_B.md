## Day by day

**2027-06-15**
- Morning: Arrival at airport at 13:00 — no morning activities scheduled
- Afternoon: Transfer from airport to Navagio Beach boutique hotel (approx. 1.5–2 hrs including ferry crossing to Zakynthos). Check in, freshen up, and take a leisurely stroll around the local area near your accommodation to get oriented. Enjoy the stunning coastal scenery around Navagio.
- Evening: Head to a nearby taverna in the Anafonitria village area for a relaxed first dinner. Explore the quiet surroundings and enjoy the warm evening air with a glass of local Verdea wine.
- Notes: Keep Day 1 light — travel fatigue is real. Prioritize settling in and hydrating. The ferry crossing from the mainland or inter-island transfer can be scenic and enjoyable.

**2027-06-16**
- Morning: Morning tour of the Acropolis and Parthenon in Athens.
- Afternoon: Lunch and a walk through the Plaka district in Athens.
- Evening: Sunset at the Temple of Olympian Zeus in Athens, then back to the hotel.

**2027-06-17**
- Morning: Rent a scooter or car and drive south to the Marathonisi Turtle Island area near Laganas Bay. Take a short boat ride to Marathonisi to see loggerhead sea turtles (Caretta caretta) nesting on the beach — a truly magical experience.
- Afternoon: Continue to Gerakas Beach on the southeastern tip of the island, one of Zakynthos's most beautiful and protected beaches. Swim and sunbathe on the golden sand. The beach is a protected nesting site so follow posted rules.
- Evening: Head into Zakynthos Town (Zante Town) for the evening. Walk along the waterfront promenade, visit Solomos Square, and enjoy the lively atmosphere.
- Notes: Car rental is recommended for this day — approx. €35–50/day. Gerakas Beach has restricted access after sunset to protect turtle nests. Return to hotel by evening.

**2027-06-18**
- Morning: Spend a relaxed morning at a quieter beach near your hotel — Porto Vromi cove is ideal. Swim, snorkel, and soak in the scenery. The calm morning waters are perfect for snorkeling around the rocky edges.
- Afternoon: Visit the Byzantine Museum and Solomos Museum in Zakynthos Town to learn about the island's rich cultural heritage, including its role in Greek literature and the devastating 1953 earthquake reconstruction.
- Evening: Attend a traditional Kantada music performance if available locally (check with your hotel), or enjoy a relaxed evening at a wine bar in Zakynthos Town sampling local Verdea white wine.
- Notes: Museum entry fees are typically €3–6 each. A relaxed cultural afternoon balances the beach-heavy days nicely.

**2027-06-19**
- Morning: Take a ferry from Zakynthos to Kefalonia (approx. 1.5 hrs). Arrive at Poros or Argostoli and check into a mid-range hotel or guesthouse. Drop your bags and head to Myrtos Beach — one of the most photographed beaches in Greece — for a morning swim.
- Afternoon: Explore the Melissani Cave Lake near Sami — an otherworldly underground lake where sunlight pierces through a collapsed ceiling and illuminates the water in brilliant blue and turquoise hues. Take the short boat ride inside the cave.
- Evening: Settle into Argostoli town for the evening. Walk along the waterfront and watch loggerhead turtles that often gather near the fishing harbor at dusk.
- Notes: Ferry tickets should be booked in advance. Melissani Cave entry is approx. €7. Myrtos Beach has limited shade — bring an umbrella and plenty of water.

**2027-06-20**
- Morning: Drive to Antisamos Beach near Sami — a stunning pebble beach with incredibly clear water, famously used as a filming location. Swim and relax in the morning calm.
- Afternoon: Visit the historic village of Assos perched on a small peninsula with a Venetian castle above. Wander the colorful village streets, swim in the small cove, and hike up to the castle ruins for panoramic views.
- Evening: Return to Argostoli for dinner. Explore the local market street and try a traditional Kefalonian pastry from a local bakery.
- Notes: Renting a car on Kefalonia is highly recommended — the island is large and public transport is limited. Approx. €40–55/day.

**2027-06-21**
- Morning: Visit Fiskardo, the only village on Kefalonia that survived the 1953 earthquake, retaining its original Venetian architecture. Stroll the charming harbor lined with colorful boats and pastel buildings.
- Afternoon: Swim at Emblisi Beach just outside Fiskardo — a secluded pebble cove with crystal-clear water. Snorkel along the rocky edges. Alternatively, kayak around the Fiskardo peninsula (kayak rental available locally).
- Evening: Enjoy a sunset dinner in Fiskardo harbor — one of the most atmospheric dining spots in the Ionian Islands.
- Notes: Fiskardo is about 50 km from Argostoli — a scenic 1-hour drive through mountains. Consider staying overnight in Fiskardo to enjoy the evening atmosphere without a long drive back.

**2027-06-22**
- Morning: Take a ferry from Kefalonia to Lefkada via Ithaca (or direct depending on schedule). Arrive in Lefkada and check into accommodation. Head straight to Nikiana Beach or Agios Ioannis Beach for a refreshing swim.
- Afternoon: Explore Lefkada Town — a charming town built largely from wood and metal to withstand earthquakes, giving it a unique architectural character. Walk across the causeway, visit the old fortress of Santa Maura, and browse the main pedestrian street.
- Evening: Enjoy the lively evening atmosphere in Lefkada Town. The main square and waterfront come alive at sunset.
- Notes: Ferry schedules between Ionian islands vary — confirm times in advance. Lefkada is connected to the mainland by a causeway, making it uniquely accessible.

**2027-06-23**
- Morning: Drive south to Porto Katsiki Beach — consistently ranked among Europe's most beautiful beaches. The dramatic white cliffs plunging into deep blue water are breathtaking. Arrive early for the best experience before it gets crowded.
- Afternoon: Continue to Egremni Beach nearby — another stunning cliff-backed beach accessible by stairs. Swim and relax. In the late afternoon, drive to Vasiliki village on the southern coast, a windsurfing hub with a lovely harbor.
- Evening: Enjoy a relaxed dinner in Vasiliki village watching windsurfers return to shore as the sun sets over the bay.
- Notes: Porto Katsiki has a small entrance fee for parking (approx. €3–5). The stairs to Egremni Beach are steep — wear good footwear. Stay hydrated in the heat.

**2027-06-24**
- Morning: Visit the beautiful village of Nydri on the east coast of Lefkada. Take a boat trip to the nearby small islands of Meganisi, Skorpios (Onassis island), and Sparti — a classic Ionian island-hopping experience with swimming stops.
- Afternoon: Return to Nydri and relax on the beach. Browse the waterfront shops and pick up local olive oil, honey, or ceramic souvenirs.
- Evening: Drive back to Lefkada Town for a final evening on the island. Enjoy a leisurely dinner and evening stroll.
- Notes: Boat trips from Nydri typically cost €15–25 per person and run most of the day. Book at the harbor in the morning.

**2027-06-25**
- Morning: Drive across the Lefkada causeway to the mainland and head toward Parga — a picturesque coastal town in Epirus (approx. 2.5 hrs drive). Check in and explore the charming Venetian castle overlooking the town.
- Afternoon: Spend the afternoon at Valtos Beach — a gorgeous sandy beach just a short boat ride or walk from Parga town. Swim and snorkel in the clear Ionian waters.
- Evening: Wander through Parga's narrow, bougainvillea-draped streets and enjoy a sunset drink from a clifftop bar overlooking the castle and islands.
- Notes: Parga is a hidden gem — less touristy than the islands. A rental car is essential for this day. Alternatively, take a bus from Lefkada to Preveza then onward to Parga.

**2027-06-26**
- Morning: Visit the ancient Oracle of Dodona (approx. 1.5 hrs inland from Parga) — one of the oldest oracles in ancient Greece, with a well-preserved ancient theater set against a dramatic mountain backdrop. A fascinating and uncrowded historical site.
- Afternoon: Return to the coast and spend the afternoon at Sarakiniko Beach near Parga — a secluded cove with calm, clear water. Alternatively, take a boat trip to the Paxos and Antipaxos islands for spectacular Blue Caves and beaches (day trip boats available from Parga harbor).
- Evening: Final evening in Parga — enjoy a long, leisurely dinner at your favorite restaurant from the trip. Toast the journey with a bottle of local wine.
- Notes: Dodona entry fee is approx. €6. The Paxos day trip is highly recommended if skipping Dodona — it's one of the most beautiful excursions in the Ionian. Book boat trips at Parga harbor the evening before.

**2027-06-27**
- Morning: Final morning in Greece — enjoy a slow breakfast and a last walk through Parga or along the beach. Pick up any final souvenirs — local olive oil, thyme honey, or Ionian sea salt make excellent gifts.
- Afternoon: Begin transfer toward your departure point. If flying from Preveza/Aktion Airport, it's approx. 45 min from Parga. If flying from Athens, allow 5–6 hrs including a connecting flight or long drive. Enjoy the scenic coastal drive.
- Evening: Departure — carry home memories of turquoise waters, fresh seafood, warm evenings, and the laid-back rhythm of the Ionian Islands.
- Notes: Allow extra time for car return if renting. Confirm your departure airport when booking — Preveza (PVK) serves the Epirus/Ionian region well. Check in online the night before to save time.
